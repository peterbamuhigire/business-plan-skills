# AI Feature Pricing & Positioning — Reference

Pointer reference for Section 07 marketing/sales-strategy. Full pricing architecture in `skills/07-marketing-sales-strategy/saas-ai-pricing-strategy/references/saas-ai-pricing-architecture.md`. This file covers positioning discipline — the claim-language alignment with the underlying capability.

## 1. The positioning ladder

| Position | Underlying capability | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| "AI-native" | The product IS AI; remove AI and the product doesn't exist | AI-revenue >70% of ARR; AI-attribution per feature; AI moat 15+; architecture is AI-orchestrated end-to-end |
| "AI-first" | AI is the primary differentiator; non-AI features support | AI-revenue 40-70%; AI moat 10-15; AI features lead the product narrative |
| "AI-powered" | AI is a material feature; non-AI features lead | AI-revenue 15-40%; AI features stated honestly as augmentation |
| "AI-enhanced" | AI improves specific features; not the primary value | AI-revenue 5-15%; AI features as bonus |
| (Don't claim AI position) | AI is internal-efficiency only | No AI marketing claim |

A plan that claims "AI-native" with 12% AI-revenue and weak AI moat will be discounted on every dimension. Match claim to evidence.

## 2. Pricing-positioning alignment

| Position | Pricing implication |
|---|---|
| AI-native | Premium pricing; outcome-based or AI-tier-only architecture; AI cost included; SLA-backed |
| AI-first | Premium tier with AI emphasis; AI-Pro upgrade for advanced features |
| AI-powered | Standard SaaS pricing; AI in higher tiers; allowance + overage |
| AI-enhanced | AI as feature in existing tiers; no separate AI pricing |

## 3. The "AI-native" claim discipline

Before claiming "AI-native":

- Could the product exist without AI? (If yes → not AI-native)
- Is the architecture AI-orchestrated end-to-end? (If no → not AI-native)
- Do customers describe the product as AI in their own language? (If no → not AI-native)
- Is AI-revenue >70% of ARR? (If no → not AI-native)
- Does AI moat score 15+ on the 7-question rubric? (If no → not AI-native)

If you can't say yes to all five, don't claim "AI-native." Use "AI-first" or "AI-powered" instead.

## 4. Freemium-on-AI cost control

Freemium AI is a structural cost risk. Disciplines that survive:

1. **Strict allowance** — e.g. 10 queries / day or 50 queries / month
2. **Cheap-model-only** for free tier — never premium models on the free tier
3. **Prompt-token compression** — system-prompt minimised; structured outputs only
4. **Cache-aggressive** — semantic cache must be priority for free-tier
5. **Conversion-funnel measurement** — free → paid conversion rate must beat free-tier AI cost
6. **Sunset policy** — free tier deprecates if conversion underperforms

A plan with "free AI to drive adoption" without these disciplines is at structural risk.

## 5. Anti-positioning failure modes

| Failure | Why it fails |
|---|---|
| "AI-native" claim with 12% AI-revenue | Investor discount on inconsistency |
| Marketing AI heavily but pricing in non-AI tiers | Customer confusion; cannibalisation of premium |
| "Free AI for all customers" without cost model | Margin disaster at scale |
| "We're the only AI [vertical] solution" when 3 competitors exist | Investor / customer pushback |
| AI claim in homepage hero but no AI tier in pricing | Mismatch between marketing and product |
| Outcome-pricing without rigorous outcome definition | Customer disputes; revenue volatility |
| Hiding model-mix from customers in tier comms | Eroded trust when discovered |

## 6. Worked example — Dairy AI SaaS positioning

**Position**: AI-first vertical SaaS for East African dairy cooperatives.

**Why "AI-first" not "AI-native"**: the cooperative-management software (member records, milk-collection logs, payments) exists without AI; AI features (Luganda advisory, anomaly detection, summarisation) are the primary differentiator vs non-AI competitors. AI-revenue is 55% of ARR. AI moat score is 12 (3 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 1).

**Marketing positioning**: "The Luganda-first AI platform for African dairy cooperatives." AI is in the hero of every page; non-AI features are listed below.

**Pricing alignment**: Standard tier includes Luganda chatbot + summarisation; Pro tier adds anomaly detection + unlimited queries. AI cost included in all paid tiers (no AI-add-on tier); Standard allowance covers median use; overage at 2.5× marginal cost.

**Free tier**: 10 Luganda chatbot queries / week; no anomaly detection; cheap-model-only routing. Sunset trigger: free → Standard conversion <8% in any quarter.

## 7. Living-plan link

Positioning is revisited:
- Quarterly: pricing + positioning review
- Annually: full repositioning analysis vs competitor + market shift
- Triggered: major new entrant; foundation-model commoditising claim; major customer feedback signal

## 8. Africa context

- "AI-first" + "local-language-first" combined positioning is a differentiator that resonates with African enterprise + regulated-vertical buyers
- Sovereign-AI positioning ("in-country AI", "data stays in [country]") is a legitimate claim that supports premium pricing in regulated verticals
- WhatsApp-first AI is an underutilised positioning lens for SMB / consumer African AI
- Trust + transparency positioning (explainability, redress, local accountability) outweighs "fastest / most-accurate" claims in many African markets
