# Posting-Model Unit Economics

Unit economics in projections should use the same classification logic that the operating system will use for actual postings.

## Required Bridge

For each product, service, subscription tier, donor-funded program, or operating segment, define:

- Revenue event.
- Direct cost event.
- COGS or cost-of-service account.
- Gross margin.
- Variable operating cost.
- Contribution margin.
- Fixed cost allocation policy, if used.
- Working-capital effect: AR days, AP days, inventory days, deferred revenue, or grant restrictions.

## Contribution Margin

Contribution margin equals revenue less variable costs directly driven by the unit sold, served, or delivered. Do not bury variable fulfilment costs in overhead if they scale with volume.

## Gross Margin

Gross margin equals revenue less COGS/cost of service. For software, include hosting, payment fees, support labour directly required to deliver the service, AI/model/tool COGS where relevant, and third-party service fees that scale with usage.

## Comparability Rule

Projected gross margin and contribution margin must be reconcilable to the same posting categories used in the embedded accounting engine. If the live system classifies a cost as COGS, the business plan should not model it as G&A.
