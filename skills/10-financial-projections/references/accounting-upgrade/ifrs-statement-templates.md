# IFRS-Aligned Statement Templates

Use these templates for projected statements unless a specific reporting regime requires another format. Default to IFRS for SMEs for East/Central African SMEs and full IFRS where the entity is publicly accountable or otherwise required.

## Income Statement

- Revenue.
- Cost of sales or cost of service.
- Gross profit.
- Other income.
- Selling and distribution expenses.
- Administrative expenses.
- Expected credit loss or bad-debt expense where relevant.
- Depreciation and amortisation, shown separately or within function with disclosure; do not double-count.
- Finance costs.
- Profit before tax.
- Income tax expense.
- Profit for the period.

## Statement Of Financial Position

- Non-current assets: PPE, intangible assets, right-of-use assets where applicable, deferred tax assets.
- Current assets: inventory, trade receivables, other receivables, tax receivable, cash and cash equivalents.
- Equity: share capital/member capital, retained earnings, reserves, donor net assets/funds where applicable.
- Non-current liabilities: borrowings, lease liabilities, deferred tax liabilities, provisions.
- Current liabilities: trade payables, payroll liabilities, tax liabilities, deferred revenue, current borrowings.

## Cash Flow Statement

Use operating, investing, and financing sections. Tie closing cash to the statement of financial position. Separate profit from cash by modelling receivables, payables, inventory, tax timing, capex, debt drawdowns, repayments, and dividends/distributions.

## Notes For Projections

- State accounting basis and standard profile.
- State revenue recognition assumptions.
- State inventory costing method: FIFO, weighted average, or specific identification. Never LIFO under IFRS.
- State depreciation methods and useful lives.
- State functional currency and FX assumptions.
- State tax assumptions and verification status.
