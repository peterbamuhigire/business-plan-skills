# SaaS Executive Summary Structure

Generic executive-summary structure is insufficient for SaaS plans. SaaS investors read the exec summary first and decide in 60 seconds whether to read further. The structure below gives them the data they want, in the order they want.

## 1. The 10-Line Summary (the must-have header)

```
Company: [Name], registered [country], founded [year]
Stage: [Pre-seed / Seed / Series A / Growth] — currently raising [amount]
Product: [one-line description of what + for whom]
Revenue model: [Subscription / Usage / Hybrid] — [SaaS / PLG / Sales-led / Marketplace]
Market: [Vertical / Sub-vertical] in [Geography]; TAM ~$X / SAM ~$Y / SOM (Y3) ~$Z
Traction: ARR $X (growing X% YoY); N customers; key logos [...]
Unit economics: LTV $X; CAC $X; LTV:CAC X:1; CAC Payback X months; Gross Margin X%
Retention: NRR X%; GRR X%; Churn X% monthly
Team: [N people]; founders [names / domain expertise]; key hires [...]
Funding ask: $X for X% equity / via [debt / DFI / blended]; use of funds [3-line]
```

The single screen / page that an investor reads first.

## 2. The Narrative Sections (after the header)

### A. The problem (and why now)
- What pain is being solved
- Why this is acute now (critical event / market shift)
- Why incumbents fail (or don't exist)
- Quantify the cost of the status quo

### B. The product
- What the product does in user terms
- Key differentiators (what makes this defensible)
- Current product status (MVP / PMF / scaling)
- AI strategy if applicable (capability, cost, moat)

### C. The market
- TAM / SAM / SOM with method (bottoms-up + tops-down + benchmark)
- Why this market is investable
- Vertical / niche positioning

### D. Business model
- Pricing architecture (tiers + dimension)
- Expansion mechanic
- GTM motion (PLG / Sales-led / Hybrid / Marketplace)
- Channel mix

### E. Traction & unit economics
- ARR / MRR with growth rate
- Customer count + key logos
- LTV, CAC, payback, LTV:CAC
- NRR, GRR, churn
- Rule of 40
- Burn multiple
- Cohort retention curve snapshot

### F. Team
- Founders' domain expertise
- Key hires
- Board / advisors
- Gaps being recruited

### G. Funding ask
- Amount
- Instrument (priced equity, SAFE, convertible, debt, DFI growth-equity)
- Valuation expectation (or "Series A norm")
- Use of funds (3-5 buckets)
- Milestones unlocked
- Runway extended

### H. Risk & mitigations (don't hide; build credibility)
- Top 3 risks
- Top 3 mitigations
- Trigger-replan events

### I. The vision (the closer)
- Where this goes if it works
- 3-5 year aspiration

## 3. The Format Rules

- **Maximum 2 pages** (or 4 if extended; never more than 6)
- **Numbers, not adjectives** — "ARR grew from $200k to $1.2M in 9 months" beats "rapid growth"
- **Specific, not generic** — "Dairy cooperatives in Western Uganda with 100-500 farmers" beats "agribusiness"
- **Visual hierarchy** — let key numbers be visually distinct
- **No jargon padding** — every word earns its place
- **End with a clear ask**

## 4. The Investor Mental Model (what they're scanning for)

When a SaaS investor reads an exec summary, they're testing:

1. **Is the team credible?** (Domain + execution evidence)
2. **Is the market big enough?** (TAM and SOM at Year 3-5)
3. **Are the unit economics real?** (LTV:CAC, payback, retention)
4. **Is there a moat?** (Why won't competitors crush this?)
5. **Is the ask reasonable?** (Round size to milestone reached)
6. **What's the exit?** (Multi-billion-dollar outcome possible?)

The exec summary must answer all six in 2 pages.

## 5. Living-Plan Cadence

| Element | Cadence | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 10-line summary header | Monthly (numbers); quarterly (narrative) | CEO + CFO |
| Full exec summary | Quarterly (if external use) | CEO |
| Investor-targeted version | At each fundraise | CEO |

## 6. Africa / Uganda Application Notes

- **Currency display**: present in local currency + USD where the audience is mixed.
- **Comparables**: cite African comparable rounds / exits (Briter Bridges; Disrupt Africa) — Western comparables don't translate.
- **DFI / patient-capital framing**: if pitching DFI, surface ESG / impact alongside commercial; if pitching VC, lead with commercial.
- **Founder bios**: surface diaspora / international experience where present (creates credibility); also surface local-context expertise.
- **Risk transparency**: African investors expect specific FX / regulatory / payment-rail risks named explicitly; hiding them backfires.
- **Vision**: pan-African vision is the default; "Become the [category] leader for Sub-Saharan Africa" is the credible long-term framing for most plays.
