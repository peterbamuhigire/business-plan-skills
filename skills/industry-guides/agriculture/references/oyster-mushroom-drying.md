# Oyster Mushroom Cultivation and Drying

**Source attribution:** Independently written synthesis of the Agriculture
Sector profile labelled `MAKING DRIED OYSTER MUSHROOMS` in the *Compendium of Investment
and Business Opportunities, Vol. 2* (Uganda Investment Authority), PDF page 66.
The source was inspected during the Wave 2 audit. Its tracked raw extraction is
removed; this reference is not a transcription and does not reproduce the
source table.

## When to use

Load this reference for a plan that grows oyster mushrooms and sells a dried
product. The existing poultry reference discusses mushroom substrate as an
integration option; this file adds the post-harvest and enterprise-separation
logic that the raw profile made relevant.

## Operating model

Treat mushroom production and drying as one coordinated value chain with
separate controls:

1. Prepare and validate the substrate and growing environment.
2. Manage hygiene, humidity, contamination, harvest timing, and batch records.
3. Move harvested mushrooms through a clean drying step with an approved
   product specification.
4. Cool, pack, label, store, and distribute the dried product without
   reintroducing moisture or contamination.

The source profile identified a tunnel dryer and delivery vehicle as relevant
equipment. It did not establish a current yield, price, energy cost, shelf-life
test, or regulatory conclusion. Treat each as a **gap** until verified for the
target market and product format.

## Planning checks

- Prove a buyer and product specification before scaling the growing room.
- Separate fresh-product and dried-product assumptions; they have different
  yield, packaging, storage, and working-capital behaviour.
- Cost substrate, spawn, labour, utilities, drying losses, packaging, testing,
  transport, rejects, and unsold stock separately.
- Keep a batch record from substrate inputs through harvest, drying, packing,
  and delivery.
- Introduce the enterprise only when the operator can staff and control it
  separately from any poultry operation.
