# Grain Grocery and Small-Scale Grain Handling

**Source attribution:** Independently written synthesis of the Agriculture Sector
profile labelled `ESTABLISHING A GRAIN GROCERY` in the *Compendium of Investment and
Business Opportunities, Vol. 2* (Uganda Investment Authority), PDF page 10.
The source was inspected during the Wave 2 audit. Its tracked raw extraction is
removed; this reference is not a transcription and does not reproduce the
source table.

## When to use

Load this reference when a plan combines grain buying or aggregation with a
grocery, cleaning, grading, weighing, packing, or resale operation. Load the
retail operating and agribusiness finance references alongside it; this file
does not replace either discipline.

## Operating model

Treat the business as a choice between two linked models:

1. A retail model buys finished, compliant stock for resale.
2. An aggregation and handling model buys raw grain, tests and grades it,
   cleans or packs it, stores it, then sells to identified buyers.

Do not mix the models in one forecast. The second model adds handling loss,
quality variation, equipment downtime, storage exposure, packaging, and
transport decisions.

## Planning workflow

1. Name the grain types, buyer specifications, purchase season, storage
   conditions, and intended sales channels.
2. Validate suppliers and buyer demand before buying stock. Record quality,
   moisture, contamination, delivery, payment, and rejection terms.
3. Design the receiving, inspection, cleaning, grading, weighing, packing,
   storage, dispatch, and returns flow. Keep rejected lots traceable.
4. Model procurement, handling, packaging, transport, shrinkage, labour,
   finance cost, and working capital separately from gross sales.
5. Reconcile physical stock to the records at a defined cadence. Investigate
   unexplained weight loss, damage, contamination, or unrecorded withdrawals.

## Evidence boundary

The source profile named transport, cleaning, grading, packing, and weighing
equipment, but its historical financial table is not retained here. The source
does not establish current prices, demand, quality limits, licensing, or a
viable margin for a present plan. Those are **gaps** until verified for the
target country, season, buyer, and scale.
