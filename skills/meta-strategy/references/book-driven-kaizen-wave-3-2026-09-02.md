# Book-driven Kaizen Wave 3: business-plan application

Use this reference to improve plans, models, strategy, pitches, nonprofit plans, and
execution frameworks. It synthesises the supplied books without storing their text.

Owning skill: [Kaizen owner](../kaizen-improvement-system/SKILL.md).

## Transfer controls

- Start with a customer/beneficiary job, value proposition, audience, and decision; connect
  each initiative to an outcome, capacity assumption, cash implication, and counter-metric.
- Make the operating model reproducible: roles, handoffs, service standards, experiments,
  franchise/replication boundaries, supply constraints, and failure recovery.
- Treat forecasts and growth tests as hypotheses. Define baseline, sample/observation plan,
  exploration versus exploitation rule, guardrails, uncertainty, and a stop/rollback rule.
- Use dashboard-style summaries with context and exceptions; never substitute vanity metrics
  for validated economics, delivery evidence, or customer outcomes.

## Boundaries

Books supply heuristics, not current market, tax, legal, platform, finance, or technology
authority. Verify volatile claims through Digital Research and Chwezi Accounting where
applicable. Do not promise SEO placement, infer causal lift from weak experiments, or treat
AI examples as proof of capability. Mark unsupported assumptions `NOT_ASSESSED`.

## Measures and routing

Track assumption validation rate, forecast error, contribution margin/cash impact, capacity
utilisation, qualified conversion, retention, experiment regret or lift, and decision latency.
Route to market validation, financial modelling, living-plan governance, analytics, and
implementation-capacity skills.

## Currentness gate

For every live claim record primary source, access date, freshness class, support, uncertainty,
and review trigger before it enters a plan or model.
