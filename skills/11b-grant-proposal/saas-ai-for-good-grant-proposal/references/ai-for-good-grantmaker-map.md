## 4. Targeting discipline

1. Match grantmaker thesis to your theory-of-change before writing
2. Read 3-5 recent grants by the grantmaker; identify pattern
3. Tailor language + emphasis to their rubric
4. Avoid generic "AI for good" framing — make the SDG / outcome linkage specific
5. Build the M&E plan with the grantmaker's reporting format in mind
6. Plan the sustainability narrative — commercial + institutional adoption + open-source community

## 5. Anti-patterns

- "AI will improve outcomes" without theory-of-change
- Generic proposal copy-pasted across funders
- Training-data provenance vague
- Sustainability via "Series A" only (circular logic)
- M&E afterthought
- Reach without disaggregation
- Capacity-building as line item without mechanism
