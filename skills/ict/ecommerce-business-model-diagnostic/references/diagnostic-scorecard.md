# Diagnostic Scorecard

## Maturity Scale

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Not evidenced | No reliable evidence or activity. |
| 1 | Ad hoc | Activity exists but depends on one person, manual work, or undocumented practice. |
| 2 | Basic | Repeatable practice exists with partial data or controls. |
| 3 | Managed | Practice is documented, measured, and owner-led. |
| 4 | Scalable | Practice can support cross-border growth with clear controls and metrics. |

## Domains

| Domain | Evidence to request | Typical risks |
|---|---|---|
| Digital operations | Storefront, app, social-commerce process, order workflow, analytics. | Manual fulfilment, poor mobile UX, weak uptime, no funnel data. |
| Data sharing | Customer, order, inventory, consent, and reporting data flows. | No data dictionary, weak consent, inconsistent records. |
| Governance | Ownership, decisions, policies, roles, controls. | Founder bottleneck, no delegation, unclear accountability. |
| Marketing | Channels, campaigns, CAC, content, conversion, retention. | Vanity metrics, no attribution, generic positioning. |
| Logistics and fulfilment | Delivery partners, SLAs, returns, stock, packaging. | Unreliable delivery, no returns policy, stockouts. |
| Customer management | Support channels, complaints, refunds, trust signals. | No response SLAs, weak proof, unmanaged complaints. |
| Financial management | Pricing, margins, cash cycle, records, reconciliations. | Margin unknown, working-capital strain, poor records. |
| Regulatory compliance | Registration, tax, data protection, consumer protection, payments. | Missing consent, unclear duties, weak terms and privacy notices. |

## Evidence Rules

- Use company data first.
- Use public evidence to verify existence, channel quality, and customer-facing claims.
- Use external benchmarks only as labelled context.
- Mark unverifiable claims as assumptions.
- Do not score above 2 without documentary or system evidence.

## Binding Constraint Logic

A binding constraint is the bottleneck that most limits growth even if other issues improve. Test candidates against:

1. Would fixing this unlock more growth than fixing other issues?
2. Does it block cross-border entry directly?
3. Is it within the TA programme's influence?
4. Can progress be measured in 90 days?
