# Company Diagnostic Report Template

## 1. Company Snapshot

- Company name, country, year established, model, channels, products, target markets.
- Diagnostic dates and data sources.
- Confidentiality classification.

## 2. Business Model

- Revenue model and key customer segments.
- Value proposition and proof points.
- Current channels and partnerships.
- Main cost drivers and operating dependencies.

## 3. Domain Scorecard

Use the maturity scorecard. For each domain include:

- Score.
- Evidence.
- Main finding.
- Risk or opportunity.
- Immediate recommendation.

## 4. Cross-Border Readiness

- Target market fit.
- Trust and proof gaps.
- Payment and logistics readiness.
- Compliance readiness.
- Customer support readiness.

## 5. Binding Constraint

State the binding constraint in one sentence, then explain evidence, implication, and what would change if fixed.

## 6. Priority Improvement Plan

| Priority | Action | Owner | Evidence | Effort | Cost level | Dependency | 90-day milestone |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 7. Donor-Safe Summary

Use anonymised or approved wording. Do not disclose confidential sales, margin, customer, supplier, or platform-security data unless explicitly cleared.
