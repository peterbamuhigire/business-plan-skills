# NGO Cyber-Resilience Investment Conditions

This reference is a self-contained synthesis prepared from Stéphane Duguin,
*Cybersecurity for NGOs: Attack Prevention and Threat Response*.

## What a fundable plan must show

1. Mission and people most exposed to harm.
2. Critical systems, identities, data, domains, partners, and dependencies.
3. Likely threats such as phishing, ransomware, spyware, impersonation, and information manipulation.
4. A proportionate control package with owners, evidence, training, and fallback.
5. Incident response, evidence preservation, trusted communications, and staged recovery.
6. A partner or collective-defence route where the NGO cannot operate alone.

Separate policy existence from tested operation. Stage funding around reduced
mission risk and recovery proof, not around purchasing a named tool.
