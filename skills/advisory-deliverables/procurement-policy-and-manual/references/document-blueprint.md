# Procurement Policy & Manual — Document Blueprint

Reusable architecture for a Procurement Policy and a Procurement & Disposal Manual, grounded in the PPDA Act 2003 (as amended) / PPDA Regulations 2023 framework and real Ugandan NGO procurement practice (UCOBAC, MCLD, IMAU). Use the chapter→source map so legal and control substance comes from existing skills, never improvised.

## Regime first — it sets the rules

| Regime | Who | Thresholds & methods | Authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — PPDA public** | Central government, agencies, local governments | **Statutory** (PPDA Regulations 2023 — harmonised central+local since 5 Feb 2024) — put current bands in the dated Threshold Schedule | PPDA Act 2003 (as amended) + PPDA Regulations 2023; `uganda-public-sector-pfm.md` for the finance interface |
| **B — Non-PPDA** | NGOs/CSOs, SMEs, private | **Organisation-set** thresholds + any binding donor rules | Entity policy + donor manuals (World Bank, USAID ADS, EU/GIZ, AfDB, UN) |

Most NGO/project clients are Regime B with one or more donor overlays. **Stricter rule wins** when entity policy and a donor rule differ.

## Two documents, one source

- **Procurement Policy** — short, board-owned (principles, authority, thresholds, ethics, committee mandates). 10–25 pages.
- **Procurement & Disposal Manual** — long procedures with forms. 40–100 pages.

## Methods-and-thresholds framework (structure, not numbers)

Define a ladder of methods keyed to value, lowest to highest:

1. **Direct / micro procurement** — below the quotation threshold; single source with justification.
2. **Request for quotations (RFQ)** — ≥3 quotations compared on a bid-analysis form; the standard small-value method (NGO practice and PPDA low bands).
3. **Restricted / selective bidding** — shortlist from the supplier database.
4. **Open competitive bidding** — advertised; the default above the open-bidding threshold.
5. **Single-source / direct contracting** — only with documented justification (emergency, sole supplier, continuity) and higher-level approval.
6. **Framework / call-off arrangements** — for recurring needs.

For Regime A, the monetary bands that trigger each method are **statutory** — record them only in the dated Threshold Schedule with a citation to the current PPDA Regulations, never in the body. For Regime B, the bands are board-set parameters; propose defaults from NGO practice (e.g. RFQ with ≥3 quotations above a client threshold; open bidding above a higher one) flagged "to be board-approved."

**No splitting:** prohibit dividing a requirement to stay under a threshold — state it as an explicit rule in chapters 4 and 5.

## Committee & separation pattern (every manual defines these)

- **User department** raises the requirement and specifications — cannot evaluate or award.
- **Procurement function / PDU** runs the process, maintains the supplier database and records — cannot approve or maintain inventory.
- **Evaluation Committee** evaluates against published criteria.
- **Contracts / Procurement Committee** awards and signs — the end user never selects the supplier or signs the contract.
- **Accounting Officer / CEO** provides overall authority within delegated limits; the **Board** owns the top threshold.

Render the authorisation thresholds and the committee composition as tables.

## Cost-eligibility & value rules

- **Lowest responsive/evaluated bid** is the default award; best-value/total-cost-of-ownership may win only with a documented reason on file.
- For donor work, apply the **Reasonable / Allocable / Allowable** test and maintain a list of unallowable costs.

## Parameterisation rule

Every figure the entity must choose is a **parameter with an owner and a review date**, presented as a default-to-be-approved — except statutory PPDA thresholds, which live in the dated **Threshold Schedule** and are verified against the current PPDA Regulations at issue. The body references "the applicable threshold per the Threshold Schedule," never a number.
