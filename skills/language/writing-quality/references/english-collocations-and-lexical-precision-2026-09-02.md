# English Output Overlay - Business Plans

[Owning skill](../SKILL.md) | Canonical study: `C:\wamp64\www\digital-research-skills\docs\continuous-improvement\english-collocations-and-lexical-precision-2026-09-02.md`

Use this overlay after business-plan structure and evidence checks. Write like a
highly educated professional: polished, exact, warm where useful, and restrained;
never pompous, archaic, or decorated for its own sake.

- Default to British English and neutral East African professional English unless the brief selects another locale.
- Choose the precise ordinary word first. Check meaning, connotation, grammatical frame, collocation, and register before using advanced vocabulary.
- Pair every material commercial claim with evidence, mechanism, assumption, comparison, limit, or explicit inference. Calibrate words such as `substantial`, `leading`, and `robust` to the proof.
- Preserve financial, legal, market, product, and institutional terms exactly when they are approved or source-defined.
- Use idioms sparingly and only when the audience, genre, translation, and decision context support them. Replace them with literal wording in models, assumptions, risks, and commitments.
- Proof agreement, articles, tense, pronouns, prepositions, parallelism, modifiers, spelling, punctuation, and sentence boundaries.

Review record: audience, purpose, locale, terms checked, claim-proof map, collocation
decisions, revision status, proof/read-aloud status, gaps, reviewer, and date.

The two supplied books provide durable language concepts, not current finance or
market authority. Do not reproduce their lists or model text; current claims still
go through Digital Research source evaluation and verification.
