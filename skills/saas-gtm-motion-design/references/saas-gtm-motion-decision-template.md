# SaaS GTM Motion Decision Template

## 1. The GTM Motion Master Matrix

| GTM Motion | ACV Range | Cycle | Methodology | Pricing | Sales Team Cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Self-service (DIY / B2C / micro-SMB) | <$500 | minutes | DIY | self-serve, transparent | minimal |
| Transactional inside-sales | $500-$5k | <30 days | Transactional | self-serve + sales-assist | low |
| Solution sales (SMB / MM) | $5k-$25k | 30-90 days | Solution | published, simple | moderate |
| Consultative enterprise | $25k-$250k | 6-18 mo | Consultative | custom, complex | high |
| Provocative / strategic enterprise | $250k+ | 6-12 mo | Provocative | custom, value-based | very high |
| PLG → Enterprise (dual funnel) | $0-$250k | varies | Hybrid | freemium → custom | hybrid |
| Marketplace | platform fee | viral | Two-sided | take-rate | varies |
| Channel-partner / Reseller | varies | varies | Partner-led | wholesale margin | low direct, high partner mgmt |

## 2. PLG Suitability Decision (the four-test)

A product is suitable for PLG only if:
1. **Time-to-value < 10 minutes** — a new user gets to "aha moment" in their first session
2. **Self-service onboarding** — no human required for setup
3. **Bottoms-up adoption pattern** — individual user / team can adopt without enterprise procurement
4. **Viral or expansion-friendly architecture** — single user spreads to team naturally (Loom shares; Figma invites; Calendly meetings link to the brand)

If any fail, the product needs sales-assisted GTM.

## 3. The Sales-Led Suitability Decision

Sales-led is required when:
- ACV >$25k (sales economics work)
- Buying committee >3 stakeholders
- Custom configuration / integration required
- Compliance / security review required (SOC2 / ISO27001 / data residency)
- Customer has not recognised the problem (provocative)

## 4. The Hybrid (Dual-Funnel) Model

Best-in-class SaaS (HubSpot, Slack, Atlassian, Notion) operate two parallel motions:

```
PLG FUNNEL                                                 SALES-LED FUNNEL
┌──────────────────────┐                                  ┌──────────────────────┐
│ Anonymous traffic    │                                  │ Outbound prospecting  │
│ Free signup          │                                  │ ABM target accounts   │
│ Self-serve activation│                                  │ Conferences / events  │
│ Self-upgrade to paid │                                  │ Referrals / network  │
└──────────┬───────────┘                                  └───────────┬──────────┘
           │                                                          │
           │ At usage / team-size threshold, qualifying signal         │
           └───────────────► Sales-assisted ◄─────────────────────────┘
                                  │
                                  │ Custom enterprise close
                                  ▼
                          Enterprise customer
                          (with PLG users in the org as champions)
```

Operating principles:
- PLG funnel feeds enterprise sales (free users become enterprise advocates)
- Enterprise sales feeds PLG (enterprise contracts include team licences that PLG-style activate)
- Marketing operates one brand, two playbooks
- Pricing must allow seamless transition (free → paid SMB → enterprise tier)

## 6. Worked Example — Ugandan Vertical SaaS GTM Choice

**Product:** dairy-cooperative SaaS, ACV UGX 9-36M ($2,500-$10,000) annually, target = 200 customers in 3 years.

**Customer state analysis:**
- Cooperatives know they have data-management pain (recordkeeping is a known issue)
- They don't know solution exists or how it works
- Buying committee: chair + secretary + finance officer (3 stakeholders)
- Procurement: governance meeting decision

**Motion decision:** **Solution sales with channel-partner amplification**
- Solution sales motion (cycle 60-90 days, ACV $2.5-10k, 5-8 deals/month/AE)
- Channel partners: dairy-extension officers (paid 10% commission), cooperative federations, MAAIF / Local Government extension network
- PLG element: free version for individual farmer members (drives cooperative adoption from below)
- Marketing: radio (Bukedde, CBS), WhatsApp groups (Dairy Cooperative WhatsApp), in-person extension officer training events

**Why not pure PLG:** the buyer is the cooperative governance, not the individual farmer. Free farmer adoption is signal, but the sale is to the cooperative.

**Why not enterprise consultative:** ACV doesn't support 12-month cycles; cooperatives aren't enterprise.

**Team composition (Year 2 at 70 customers):**
- 1 sales lead (founder transition)
- 2 AEs (solution sales, target $1M ARR each by Y3)
- 1 SDR (outbound + channel coordination)
- 1 channel manager (partner network)
- 1 CSM (high-touch, especially for first 6 months of each customer)
- 1 marketing-content lead (radio + WhatsApp + extension materials)

## 7. Africa-Specific Motion Considerations

- **Sales cycle inflation**: 50–100% longer than US benchmarks for similar ACV
- **WhatsApp at every stage** — outbound, qualification, demos, contracts, support
- **Channel partners** disproportionately important — local SIs, sector associations, extension officers
- **Public-sector** has 6–18 month procurement cycles regardless of ACV; build separate motion or skip the segment
- **Donor-funded** customers operate on donor budget cycles; sync the motion calendar
- **In-person remains powerful** — budget travel; conferences (SaaStr Africa, Africa Tech Week, GITEX Africa, AfricaCom)
- **Multi-language sales** is a moat — at least 2 local languages for the AE / SDR team
