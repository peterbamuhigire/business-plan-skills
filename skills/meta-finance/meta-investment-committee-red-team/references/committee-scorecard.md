# Committee scorecard

Parent: [Investment Committee Red-Team](../SKILL.md)

Score each applicable criterion from 0 to 4: `0` contradicted or absent, `1` material weakness,
`2` qualified, `3` supported, `4` strong and reproducible. `Not assessed` is not converted to zero
or a pass; it blocks when the criterion is mandatory.

| Criterion | Common weight | Observable acceptance |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Decision and funding logic | 15 | Ask, instrument, use and milestone are explicit and reconcile |
| Customer and market evidence | 15 | Method, source, countercase and demand evidence support the case |
| Commercial/unit economics | 15 | Revenue, margin, acquisition, retention and sensitivity reconcile |
| Model integrity and cash | 20 | Formula map passes; assumptions, scenarios, balance and funding tie |
| Delivery and management | 10 | Owners, capacity, dependencies and recovery actions are evidenced |
| Risk and regulatory gates | 10 | Material gates have current sources and resolved stop conditions |
| Evidence and governance | 10 | Claims, versions, approvals, conflicts and exceptions are traceable |
| Audience-specific criterion | 5 | The audience overlay's controlling condition passes |

Blockers take precedence: missing mandatory artefact, failed formula/balance check, unsupported
load-bearing claim, unresolved eligibility, unassessed licence perimeter, cash shortfall without a
funded response, or an unauthorised certification claim.

Recommendation bands apply only after blockers:

- `decline`: fatal mandate, integrity or economics conflict;
- `defer`: remediable blocker or mandatory criterion not assessed;
- `conditional-progress`: no blocker and weighted result at least 70%, with explicit conditions;
- `progress-for-real-review`: no blocker, result at least 85%, and no material condition outstanding.
