# Committee review framework

Parent: [Investment Committee Red-Team](../SKILL.md)

## Roles and rounds

| Role | Controlling question | Evidence expected |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chair | What exact decision is authorised now? | Mandate, ask, conditions and conflicts |
| Commercial lead | Who pays, why now, and what evidence defeats the countercase? | Customer evidence, market method, pipeline and competition |
| Operating lead | Can the team deliver at the modelled cost and pace? | Capacity, dependencies, owners, controls and recovery tests |
| Finance lead | Do formulae, cash, balance, funding and downside reconcile? | Formula map, assumptions, three-statement/tie-out checks and scenarios |
| Risk/regulatory lead | Which gate can lawfully or operationally stop launch? | Sector screens, competent sources and professional-review state |
| Impact/MEL lead | Are outcomes additional, measurable and attributable? | Theory of change, indicators, baseline, safeguards and evaluation plan |

Run the chair round first and synthesis last. Other roles challenge independently before seeing the
aggregate score. Record conflicts rather than manufacturing consensus.

## Audience overlays

- **Lender:** repayment source, debt-service coverage, security, covenant headroom, working capital
  and downside recovery.
- **DFI:** lender questions plus development additionality, environmental/social risk, integrity,
  procurement and reporting capacity.
- **VC:** growth evidence, retention, unit economics, moat, capital efficiency, governance,
  valuation and dilution.
- **Grant:** eligibility, additionality, theory of change, MEL, allowable budget, safeguarding,
  sustainability and restricted-fund controls.
- **Owner-board:** cash survival, owner capacity, control gaps, break-even choices and reversible
  staging; no external-funder theatre.

## Stop and recovery

Stop the verdict when inputs are not versioned, an external fact is unsourced, formulae have broken
references, balance checks fail, or a regulatory stop condition remains. Recovery requires the
named owner to supply the missing evidence and a re-run against the new version.
