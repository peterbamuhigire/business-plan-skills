# Worked simulations

Parent: [Investment Committee Red-Team](../SKILL.md)

## DFI debt case

Input: complete plan and model, but the environmental screen is `not-assessed`. Decision: `defer`,
even if projected coverage is strong. Evidence request: site/activity classification and competent
authority route. Acceptance: gate status and its cost/timing effects are recorded in the model and
implementation schedule.

## VC case

Input: growth case with sourced customer interviews, but retention is inferred from a small pilot
and valuation assumes mature comparables. Decision: `conditional-progress` only if no blocker is
present; require cohort evidence and a valuation range showing the dilution countercase. Do not
invent a market multiple.

## Grant case

Input: clear activities and budget, but beneficiary baseline and restricted-fund controls are
absent. Decision: `defer`. Evidence requests: baseline method, indicator definitions, procurement
route and fund-accounting ownership. Narrative quality cannot compensate for the missing controls.
