# Accounting Finance Quality Gate

Use this gate before a plan is called bankable, investor-ready, or board-ready.

## 1. Statement Integrity

- Income statement: revenue drivers, COGS, gross margin, operating expenses, depreciation, finance cost, tax, net income.
- Cash flow: collections, supplier payments, payroll/tax timing, capex, debt drawdown, repayments, dividends, owner injections, closing cash.
- Balance sheet: cash, receivables, inventory, PPE, payables, tax balances, loans, equity, retained earnings.
- Link checks: closing cash ties to cash flow, retained earnings ties to profit, PPE ties to capex/depreciation, debt ties to drawdown/repayment, inventory ties to purchases/COGS.

## 2. Revenue And Cost Logic

- Revenue is built from price, volume, customers, usage, capacity, contracts, route coverage, seats, transactions, or utilization.
- COGS is tied to actual delivery inputs: materials, labor, packaging, freight, platform fees, payment charges, wastage, and yield loss.
- Fixed and variable costs are separated. Contribution margin and break-even are calculated where relevant.
- Capacity constraints are explicit: premises, equipment, staff hours, fleet, stock turns, production yield, delivery slots, or service utilization.
- Seasonality, credit sales, refunds, discounts, shrinkage, bad debt, and customer churn are not hidden.

## 3. Management Accounting

- Identify cost centers, profit centers, project codes, branches, products, routes, and customer segments needed for management decisions.
- Include budgets and flexible variance review for businesses with monthly operations.
- Use standard costing where repeatable production or service delivery exists.
- Use CVP and margin of safety for pricing, scale-up, and loan-sizing decisions.
- Include KPIs that match responsibility: gross margin, contribution margin, stock turns, labor utilization, collection days, payable days, wastage, yield, cash conversion cycle.
- Where overhead is material, explain the cost driver and allocation basis rather than spreading costs evenly by habit.
- Where branches, subsidiaries, or departments trade internally, explain transfer pricing and avoid double-counting profit.
- For board or investor dashboards, pair financial outcomes with leading operating indicators so the plan shows controllability, not only results.

## 4. Accounting And Tax Readiness

- State accounting basis and reporting cadence.
- Explain how source documents become reliable books: invoicing, receipts, purchases, inventory, payroll, bank/mobile money, tax, reconciliations, close.
- Include VAT/sales tax, PAYE/payroll tax, withholding, corporate tax, social security, duties, and statutory filing where country context requires it.
- For asset-heavy businesses, include fixed-asset register, depreciation, maintenance, insurance, impairment/disposal logic.
- For inventory businesses, state costing method, stock-count cadence, slow-moving stock review, shrinkage assumptions, and write-off controls.
- For debt, leases, or long-term payables, separate principal and interest, show repayment timing, and ensure finance costs are not hidden in operating expenses.
- For acquisitions, subsidiaries, joint ventures, or group structures, state consolidation logic, ownership, intercompany balances, goodwill/intangible assumptions, and minority interests where relevant.

## 5. Financial Controls

- Segregation of duties covers sales, collections, procurement, payments, stock, payroll, journals, write-offs, and reconciliations.
- Approval thresholds and maker-checker controls exist for high-risk transactions.
- Bank/mobile money reconciliation is frequent enough for transaction volume.
- Period close has named responsibilities and evidence.
- Management dashboards reconcile to books, not separate spreadsheets.

## 6. Investor And Lender Readiness

- Funding request equals use-of-funds schedule.
- Repayment source or investor value creation is explicit.
- DSCR, current ratio, gross margin, net margin, runway, and cash conversion cycle are calculated where relevant.
- Sensitivity variables are the actual economic drivers, not cosmetic percentages.
- Historical records or reconstructed records support starting assumptions.
- Appendix evidence supports high-stakes claims: contracts, quotations, licences, tax records, bank statements, payroll, leases, supplier terms, and collateral.

## Severity Labels

- Critical: breaks funding logic, statement integrity, legal/tax compliance, or repayment/value thesis.
- High: materially weakens credibility or due-diligence readiness.
- Medium: fix improves clarity, controls, or model usability.
- Low: formatting, wording, or minor presentation issue.
