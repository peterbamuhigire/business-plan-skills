| # | Metric | Target | Score |
|---|---|---|---|

## 5. Overall Readiness Band

| Score | Band | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 80-100 | Investor-ready | Outreach to target investors now |
| 65-79 | Near-ready | Close named gaps in 1-3 months |
| 50-64 | 6-12 months from ready | Install operating discipline; come back when metrics improve |
| <50 | Not yet | Focus on fundamentals; PMF first |

## 6. Investor Matching by Readiness Score

| Score | Suitable investor types |
|---|---|
| 80+ | Series A funds, growth-equity, strategic acquirers |
| 70-79 | Seed funds, post-seed funds, accelerators with check |
| 60-69 | Angels, friends-and-family, micro-funds, TinySeed-style |
| <60 | Bootstrap, grants, accelerator programs (without immediate cheque) |

## 7. African Adjustments

- Reduce Rule of 40 expectation by 5–10 in Years 1–3
- Reduce CAC Payback strictness by 30–50% (longer cycles)
- Increase tolerance on customer concentration (smaller TAM); disclose plan
- Add ESG / impact scorecard if DFI is in scope
- Audit by IFRS-licensed firm preferred for >$1M raises
