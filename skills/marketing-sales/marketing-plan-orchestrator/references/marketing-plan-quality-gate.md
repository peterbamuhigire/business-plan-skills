# Marketing plan quality gate and rubric

Parent: [Marketing Plan Orchestrator](../SKILL.md).

When to read: before releasing any standalone marketing plan, and when
reviewing business-plan section 07 at the depth of a marketing plan. The gate
is read-only: it scores, lists blockers and names fixes; it does not rewrite.
Run it after `writing-quality` and before `anti-ai-slop` and `ai-slop-audit`.

## 1. Blockers (any one fails the plan)

1. An objective fails the [SMART quality test](smart-objectives-builder.md) or
   has no baseline, place, owner or date.
2. The plan budget does not equal the P&L marketing line, or the demand-to-cash
   bridge does not reconcile with capacity and the model.
3. A market size, benchmark, platform fact or legal statement has no source or
   register claim ID and is not labelled as an estimate or assumption.
4. Channels were chosen without considering alternatives (no Bullseye or
   equivalent) or without tracking in place.
5. Scale spend is committed before research, an approved plan and a capped
   test (sequencing gate).
6. Direct marketing or data collection is planned without the data-protection
   line (registration, consent, objection and opt-out handling) for each
   jurisdiction.
7. The plan is called bankable, viable, investor-ready or compliant without the
   engine's evidence gates.
8. No control plan: programmes lack owner, reviewer, KPI, tolerance band or
   corrective action.

## 2. Scored rubric (0–4 per dimension)

Score 0 = absent, 1 = generic, 2 = present but unevidenced or unreconciled,
3 = evidenced and specific, 4 = evidenced, specific, reconciled and decision-ready.

| # | Dimension | What a 4 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Situation analysis | 3–6 key drivers with implications; five forces with evidence; EFAS/IFAS/SFAS on one scale; TOWS strategies carried forward |
| 2 | Customer understanding | Target market passes the four checks with justification; customer insights report with method and sample; buying situations and sensitivities drive choices |
| 3 | Market sizing | Market ladder; top-down and bottom-up with the gap explained; sanity check |
| 4 | Competition | Category alternatives included; weighted grids with customer-derived weights; barriers with duration |
| 5 | Positioning and message | Positioning statement with a checkable reason to believe; one or two buyer-benefit answers; two or three message themes with proof |
| 6 | Objectives | Full cascade, reconciled arithmetic, lines in the sand with sources, location-calibrated |
| 7 | Strategy | Explicit choices and rejected alternatives; mix decisions per P; push/pull balance; growth route |
| 8 | Channels | Bullseye across 19 channels; three test cards; focus rule; decay watch |
| 9 | Advertising and media | Budget triangulated with floor; reach, frequency and flighting; CPIM table; creative brief; message test |
| 10 | Digital and content | Channel roles and measurement set-up; clean handoff to the social-media engine; consent handling |
| 11 | Sales | Structure, ratios from records, qualification rules; B2B contact economics and capacity check |
| 12 | Retention and economics | CLV method; CAC ceiling or allowable cost per lead; break-even response rates for direct campaigns; retention actions |
| 13 | Budget and reconciliation | Two-phase budget; monthly by vehicle; equals the P&L line; cash timing shown |
| 14 | Implementation | Calendar with owners; critical path; not-doing list |
| 15 | KPIs and control | OMTM and guard-rails; good-metric test passed; control table; operating cadence |
| 16 | Risk | Register with triggers and pre-agreed responses; compliance risks |
| 17 | Location and scope fit | Scope declared; payment, media, language, season and legal realities applied per region |
| 18 | Evidence discipline | Every number classed and sourced; register current; NOT_ASSESSED items listed |
| 19 | Language | Specific, human, professional British English; passes the phrase-bank tone rules and anti-slop gate |

**Release thresholds:** no blockers; total at least 60 of 76; no dimension
below 2; dimensions 6, 13, 15 and 18 at 3 or above. Record the score, the
blockers found, the fixes and the reviewer in the plan's release record. A
missing review is NOT_ASSESSED, never a pass.

## 3. Reviewer questions

- Which customers are deliberately excluded, and why?
- Why is this offer preferable to the buyer's best alternative?
- What evidence would overturn the recommendation?
- Can delivery honour the promise at the target volume?
- Where does cash run short, and how is it funded?
- What will the owner stop if the first test disappoints?
