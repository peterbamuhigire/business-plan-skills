# Marketing plan decision workpapers

Parent: [Marketing Plan Orchestrator](../SKILL.md).

## Benchmark scope

[Bain's strategy service](https://www.bain.com/consulting-services/strategy/) connects customer focus, strategic choices and execution; [McKinsey's growth service](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/growth-marketing-and-sales/how-we-help-clients) spans insight, brand, pricing, lifecycle and marketing. Opened 2026-09-22. These are their advertised capabilities, not audited comparative outcomes. Our synthesis is a plan that joins market evidence, resource choices and implementation rather than borrowing consulting vocabulary.

## Market sensing design

Choose the method from the uncertainty:

| Question | Candidate method | Limitation to record |
|---|---|---|
| Why do buyers decline or delay? | Authorised win/loss interviews and sales-record analysis | Recall, selection and interviewer bias |
| Where does the experience fail? | Observation, task study and service journey mapping | Sample/context coverage; no automatic population estimate |
| What do customers mean by quality? | Interviews, artefact elicitation and comparative concept review | Stated preference may differ from buying behaviour |
| Will the offer sell at this price? | Bounded offer/price experiment with appropriate consent and fairness | Sample, implementation and transfer to wider market |
| Which channel deserves investment? | Small comparable channel tests plus buyer research | Attribution, sales lag, novelty and unequal audiences |
| How large is the reachable opportunity? | Bottom-up segment counts, access constraints and capacity model | Definitions, source age and double counting |

Document decision, population, recruitment, sample rationale, instrument,
consent/rights, analysis, bias, uncertainty and the decision the evidence permits.
Do not fabricate interviews or treat a synthetic persona as a respondent.

## Premium positioning workpaper

Name the target buying situation, relevant alternative, benefit, distinctive
capability, inspectable proof, delivery standard and reason to pay. Distinguish
premium professional value from luxury symbolic value. A brand can be costly
without being valuable; proposed pricing remains a hypothesis until supported.

Record the service/product truth behind each promise: owner, process, capability,
capacity, quality evidence, failure remedy and cost. Include sustainability or
heritage only when provenance and operating evidence support the claim. Avoid
false scarcity, social exclusion and cosmetic luxury treatments.

## Mix and execution workpapers

| Decision | Required fields |
|---|---|
| Segment | Need, buying occasion, size evidence, access, attractiveness, fit, exclusions |
| Offer | Customer job, scope, proof, delivery, support, alternatives, lifecycle |
| Price | Value hypothesis, contribution, payment/discount terms, price test, downside |
| Distribution | Direct/partner/digital/physical routes, coverage, channel margin, conflict, service control |
| Promotion | Audience, message, evidence, channel role, cost, creative, rights, next action |
| Sales | Qualification, buying group, stage criteria, effort, cycle, response and handoff |
| Retention | Adoption, service quality, recovery, repeat purchase, referral, customer effort |
| Governance | Owner, approved budget, dependency, review cadence, escalation and stop/restart rule |

## Reconcile demand, profit and cash

Build a period-based bridge: reachable qualified audience → enquiries → accepted
opportunities → wins after sales lag → deliverable volume → revenue recognition
→ cash collection. Show retention/repeat behaviour separately and avoid counting
the same customer twice. Reconcile volume with staffing, inventory or service
capacity. Cost research, creative, media, labour, agency fees, tools, sales and
fulfilment under the finance owner's agreed policy.

Use explicit base, downside and upside assumptions; do not manufacture precise
probabilities. Calculate contribution and cash needs, and identify the variable
that changes the decision. A plan with attractive annual profit can still need
working capital before customer receipts arrive. Route accounting/tax treatment
to Chwezi; do not embed generic statutory rates in a marketing template.

Synthetic rehearsal: a team can deliver four projects in a quarter. A campaign
scenario predicts ten wins. The plan fails until capacity, timing or demand is
revised; increasing the media budget is not the default correction. These counts
are illustrative, not market evidence.

## Standards applicability and final challenge

[SBA planning guidance](https://www.sba.gov/counseling/plan-your-business/),
opened 2026-09-22, connects marketing/sales strategy with financial projections.
Use that as a planning cross-check, not a universal lender standard or a local
legal requirement. The [ICC/ESOMAR 2025 research code](https://standards.esomar.org/assets/documents/icc-esomar-code-2025.pdf)
provides an ethical reference for research design and reporting; check its
applicability alongside local law and the engagement's research obligations.

Use a register with jurisdiction, activity, audience, instrument/version,
applicability rationale, source/locator, obligation, implementation evidence,
reviewer and status. Cover advertising, privacy, professional/sector claims,
rights, accessibility, procurement and finance where relevant. Separate law,
contract, voluntary guidance and internal quality criteria. Do not claim that an
international marketing code certifies compliance in every country.

The final reviewer should answer: which customers are deliberately excluded;
why this offer is preferable to a credible alternative; what evidence could
overturn the recommendation; whether delivery can honour the promise; where
cash runs short; and what the owner will stop if the first test disappoints.
If these answers are missing, polish cannot make the plan investment-ready.
