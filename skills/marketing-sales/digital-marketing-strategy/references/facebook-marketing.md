# Facebook and Meta Marketing: Setup, Content, Advertising and Local Tactics

Use this reference when a plan relies on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger or WhatsApp Business for customer acquisition. Treat the Meta family as one ecosystem: Page, Groups, Messenger, WhatsApp, Instagram, Marketplace and Reels, managed together in Meta Business Suite. A typical journey: ad, then a WhatsApp enquiry, then payment by mobile-money link, then a Page review.

Platform features, specifications, benchmarks and prices change frequently. Every figure below is a planning assumption to re-verify with a dated source (Meta Business Help Centre, Ads Manager, DataReportal for audience size) before it enters a plan.

## 1. Fit test: is Facebook the right channel?

1. Confirm the target customer's age and location. In East Africa Facebook has long been the broadest platform for adults roughly 25 to 55, while TikTok and Instagram skew younger; verify current reach for the target country.
2. Note reasons for strong local performance: mobile-first access, telecom data bundles that favour Meta apps, WhatsApp as the everyday communication tool, active Marketplace, and Groups as trusted local information channels.
3. Decide the role of each Meta property.

| Property | Role |
|---|---|
| Facebook Page | Brand presence, content hub, advertising |
| WhatsApp Business | Enquiries, order taking, closing the sale |
| Instagram | Visual brand, younger audience (fashion, food, beauty) |
| Messenger | Enquiries and automated replies, best when tied to the Page |

Rule: run the business from a Page, never a personal profile. Personal profiles cannot advertise, lack analytics and the business inbox, and breach the terms of service.

## 2. Page setup checklist

- Category: choose the most specific one (restaurant, local business, product or service, community organisation, brand); it drives local discovery and audience suggestions.
- About: business name customers search for; short description leading with what you do and for whom; longer description with differentiators and service area; full address including neighbourhood; a WhatsApp-enabled phone number; website or WhatsApp link; kept-current opening hours; price range.
- Images: recognisable profile image at small size; cover image cropped differently on mobile, so keep key content centred; include the business name, a proof statement (years trading, customers served), the shopfront if physical; refresh at least quarterly; a short cover video is an option. Check current size specifications.
- Call-to-action button: WhatsApp message for most small businesses; message, call, book, shop or learn more as appropriate.
- Pinned post: current offer, a "what we do" video, social proof or a lead magnet ("message us the word QUOTE"); refresh monthly.
- Page transparency (creation date, managing country, ad activity) is public; consistent history builds trust.

## 3. Organic content strategy

Organic reach for business Pages is low (a small percentage of followers), but content still matters: strong posts travel beyond followers, an active Page reassures buyers checking credibility, good posts can later be boosted, and the algorithm increasingly surfaces video to non-followers.

### Format guide

| Format | Reach | Best use |
|---|---|---|
| Short video (Reels) | Highest, reaches non-followers | Awareness, top of funnel |
| Longer native video | High | Explainers, testimonials, demonstrations |
| Live video | Very high while live | Events, Q&A, launches |
| Single image | Medium | Product showcase, behind the scenes |
| Carousel | Medium | Range, steps, before and after |
| Text status | Low | Quick question or poll |
| External link post | Lowest | Avoid; put the link in a comment |
| Poll | Medium, high engagement | Audience research |
| Story (24 hours, vertical) | Followers | Daily updates, timed offers |

Plan a small number of short videos each week and measure them against images.

### Content mix
About 40 percent educational, 30 percent entertaining or relatable, 30 percent promotional. Mostly promotional feeds train followers to ignore the Page.

### Frequency (starting assumptions)
- Under about 500 followers: three to four posts a week.
- Roughly 500 to 5,000: four to five posts plus daily Stories.
- Above 5,000: five to seven posts, daily Stories, two Reels a week.
Quality beats volume; weak posts reduce future distribution.

### Timing
Commuter and lunch periods, evenings after work and weekend mornings are common peaks in East African cities. Use the Page's own "when your followers are online" data over general benchmarks.

### Ranking signals (verify current behaviour)
Positive: comments (especially threaded replies), shares, strong reactions, video watch time and completion, saves, click-through, prior relationship with the Page. Negative: engagement bait, clickbait, misinformation flags, external links in the post body.

### Copy rules
Start with the reader ("you" more than "we"); open with a question or bold statement; short paragraphs for mobile; use the audience's own language; end with one call to action ("comment PRICE", "WhatsApp to order"); link in the first comment.

## 4. Groups strategy

Groups usually get better organic distribution than Pages. Three options:
- A. Own a topic community (about the customers' interest, not the brand); limit commercial posts to about one in five.
- B. Participate in existing neighbourhood, professional and sector groups: answer questions helpfully, mention the business only when relevant, follow group rules.
- C. Run a private customer group for loyalty perks, early access, support and testimonials.

Setup: keyword-focused name; public for growth or private for exclusivity; clear description; three to five rules; pinned welcome post asking members to introduce themselves; two or three entry questions to filter spam and learn about the audience.
Engagement: questions, polls, tips and case studies rather than product posts; celebrate milestones; monthly themed threads; live Q&A; reply personally, especially in the first twelve hours. "Looking for a recommendation" threads are legitimate places for the provider to reply.

## 5. Stories and Reels

Stories: daily opening notice and special, behind-the-scenes clips, timed offers, customer shout-outs (with permission), polls and question stickers, countdowns; vertical 9:16; short cards; add a messaging link.

Reels: hook within two seconds; vertical; burned-in captions; phone-shot authenticity over studio polish; licensed or trending audio; about 15 to 30 seconds for awareness, up to about 60 for educational pieces. Remove third-party watermarks before reposting. Meta provides built-in caption, music, trimming and resizing tools.

Idea bank: product being made, day in the life, unboxing or reaction, before and after, "three things to know before buying X", price reveal ("what this service includes"), staff introductions.

## 6. Advertising

Rule of economics: can the business spend X and reliably earn X plus profit? If so, scaling is a business decision. Prove the economics with a small test first.

### Objectives
Awareness; traffic; engagement; leads; app promotion; sales. Start small businesses on leads (instant forms, no website needed) or messages (click-to-WhatsApp). Move to sales optimisation only with a tracking pixel and enough conversion volume (Meta's learning guidance is around fifty optimisation events a week; verify).

### Structure
Campaign (objective, budget), ad set (audience, placement, budget, schedule), ad (creative, copy, button). One campaign per objective; several ad sets to test audiences; several ads to test creative.

### Audience tiers
1. Custom audiences (warmest, best return): customer lists, website visitors (pixel), Page or Instagram engagers at 30/60/90/180 days, video viewers, imported WhatsApp contacts. Start by retargeting these.
2. Lookalike audiences: built from a customer list (a few hundred records or more gives better accuracy), pixel data or high-completion video viewers. Start with the closest match (1 percent), test 2 percent.
3. Interest and demographic targeting when there is no data: location (city or district), the real buying age band, gender where relevant, interests, behaviours.
Meta's automated audience expansion often matches or beats rigid manual targeting once conversion data exists; test both.

### Ad formats
Single image (about 1,200 x 628 px, little text); single video uploaded natively with captions; carousel; collection (needs a catalogue); instant lead form; messaging ads (click-to-WhatsApp is usually the strongest format for small local businesses: no website, conversation in the sales channel); Stories ads; Reels ads.
Click-to-WhatsApp setup: create a traffic or leads campaign and choose WhatsApp as the destination.

### Budget stages (illustrative; convert to plan currency and check against actual costs)
- Test: a very small daily spend to learn cost per conversation started.
- Prove: scale one ad set gradually once costs are acceptable; add a second creative and audience.
- Scale: raise the winning ad set by about 20 to 30 percent every few days; add lookalike and retargeting ad sets once there are about 100 contacts.
Never scale a losing ad. If after seven days no winner with acceptable economics emerges, pause and test new creative.

### Bidding and discipline
Lowest-cost bidding for beginners; cost cap when the maximum acceptable cost per result is known; bid cap for experts. Use daily budgets; change a running ad no more than every three to five days (edits restart learning); judge on three to seven days of data, not one day.

### Common mistakes
1. Not testing several creatives (run two or three per ad set, cut losers after five to seven days).
2. Targeting too broadly.
3. Using the Boost Post button instead of Ads Manager.
4. Judging within the first 24 hours (learning phase).
5. Ignoring public comments on ads; answer complaints daily.
6. Set-and-forget; review daily in the first two weeks.

### Creative and copy
- Images: avoid the platform's blue and white; bright contrast; faces; product in use; keep image text minimal.
- Copy: line 1 names the customer's problem or desire; lines 2 to 3 give the solution and proof; line 4 gives the offer and action.
- Headline: one specific benefit ("same-day delivery across the city" beats "quality service you can trust").
- Button matched to the goal (Send Message, Learn More, Shop Now).

### A/B testing
One variable at a time: creative, audience, offer, placement. Run five to seven days minimum. Choose winners on cost per result (lead, purchase, conversation), not reach.

## 7. Lead generation

| Approach | Strength | Weakness | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instant form | No website; pre-filled; high completion | Lower intent; needs follow-up | Services, courses, bookings |
| Click-to-WhatsApp | High intent; immediate conversation | Needs fast staff response | Most small businesses |
| Landing page | Full message control; trackable | Needs a fast mobile site | Complex offers, e-commerce |

Lead magnets: rate card or price list; free consultation with capped slots; free sample or trial; informational guide; discount code triggered by a keyword; free audit or inspection. A magnet must be valuable, easy to deliver and relevant to what is sold.

Instant form setup: higher-volume (short) or higher-intent (with review step; start with volume to gather data); clear intro; pre-filled name and phone plus one or two custom questions; privacy policy link; thank-you screen with the next step and time frame; export leads or connect to a CRM or spreadsheet. Contact every lead within about an hour; set up an instant notification.

## 8. Shop and messaging commerce

Facebook Shop is a catalogue and discovery tool. Native checkout is not available in every country, so use checkout by messaging or on the business website. Catalogue: real photos, several angles, sizes and dimensions, clear inclusions and delivery charges, collections.

Typical sales flow: customer sees a product, messages the business, confirms availability, agrees price, receives payment details, pays by mobile money or bank transfer and sends proof, the business confirms and arranges delivery. Ask for the mobile-money reference number, not only a screenshot, because screenshots can be forged.

WhatsApp Business features to configure: catalogue, quick replies, customer labels (new enquiry, paid, awaiting delivery, completed), business hours with away message, greeting message.

Marketplace: list the top ten to twenty products with clear prices and real photos; refresh weekly; reply fast (buyers contact several sellers); link listings to WhatsApp; services can be posted in the services section. Marketplace listings complement, not replace, a Shop.

## 9. Messenger

Response time is shown publicly and affects enquiry volume. Use the unified inbox. Set: instant reply (acknowledge, state response time, give the WhatsApp number), away message (hours, next reply time), four or five FAQ buttons (price range, delivery areas, payment methods, location).

Chatbot flow template for a small business: greeting; menu (get a quote, products, delivery information, talk to a person); collect need, location, phone number; promise a call-back window; hand off to a human. Tools range from Meta's built-in automation to third-party flow builders. Meta's AI reply features can answer common questions from Page and website information; escalate complex ones.

Broadcasts are permitted only to users who previously messaged and opted in (announcements, offers, events, seasonal campaigns). Never send unsolicited messages; spam reports lead to suspension.

## 10. Local adaptation

- Mobile money: include payment instructions in messages and quick replies, keep confirmation workflow strict, and watch for in-app payment rollouts.
- Language: publish mainly in English for reach and searchability, add local-language phrases naturally, and reply in the language the customer uses. Use AI for structure but localise manually.
- Hashtags: mix location, sector and community tags.
- Community groups: follow group rules; lead with help, not promotion.
- Seasonal content: national days, religious holidays, school terms, local sporting events.

## 11. Analytics

Weekly metrics: reach, impressions (impressions divided by reach is frequency), engagement rate (reactions plus comments plus shares, divided by reach; a commonly quoted good range is about 3 to 8 percent, verify), reach relative to followers, follower growth, response rate and time (targets such as 90 percent and under an hour are reasonable internal standards).

Insights to use: audience demographics against the target customer; hours followers are online; top posts (repeat winning topics and formats); video retention (a big drop in the first seconds means a weak hook).

Pixel: install on the website, verify with a helper tool, set standard events (page view, content view, add to cart, purchase); it enables retargeting, lookalikes, conversion tracking and conversion optimisation. Without a website, use the conversions integration; for messaging-led sales the "conversations started" event is the conversion.

Ad metric glossary and targets:
- CPM (cost per thousand impressions), CPC (cost per click), CTR (click-through; roughly 1 to 3 percent is typical), CPL (cost per lead; track your own), ROAS (return on ad spend; a minimum target of 3 times is a common rule of thumb), frequency (keep below about three to four; above about five causes fatigue, so change creative), quality ranking (aim above average).
Local CPM and CPC values differ widely by country, audience and season; obtain them from a live test.

Competitor research: the public ad library (see every active ad of a competitor Page), the suite's peer benchmarks, and Page-analysis tools.

## 12. Weekly content calendar template (adapt by sector)

| Day | Format | Angle | Call to action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | Image | Product or menu highlight with price | Visit or order by message |
| Tuesday | Short video | Behind the scenes | Book or enquire |
| Wednesday | Educational post | Tip relevant to the sector | Invite verification of quality |
| Thursday | Testimonial | Customer quote (permission) | Message to book |
| Friday | Promotional | Weekend offer with deadline | Message a keyword to reserve |

Daily Stories: morning opening notice and special; midday customer or product; evening last orders or poll.
Monthly: two live sessions; one poll; one testimonial compilation; one team spotlight; seasonal pieces as needed.
Batching: one weekly session (about two hours) to film two short videos, photograph products in natural light, write all captions and schedule in the free suite; about fifteen minutes daily for Stories and replies. The built-in scheduler is enough until managing several Pages.

## 13. Built-in AI tools (verify availability)

Ad copy suggestions, image generation and variations, caption suggestions, automated creative variation, audience expansion, AI replies in Messenger, Reels editing aids. AI copy is strongest in English; localise other languages by hand.

## 15. Platform monetisation (only for content-led businesses)

Relevant to media pages, education channels, entertainment and large community operators; not a core strategy for typical small businesses. Sequence: build the audience for the core business first, evaluate monetisation once the Page has substantial engaged followers, and never optimise for monetisation before customer acquisition.

- In-stream video ads: revenue share on longer videos (historically about three minutes or more) and live streams. Historical eligibility thresholds included around 10,000 followers, hundreds of thousands of minutes viewed and regular uploads, plus country eligibility and partner monetisation policy compliance; verify current terms and whether the country qualifies.
- Fan subscriptions: recurring monthly payments for exclusive posts, badges, members-only groups and live sessions; eligibility based on follower or return-viewer counts and country availability. Define a clear supporter value proposition and publish exclusive content consistently. Useful for professional expertise channels (advice, finance, agriculture, coaching). Model revenue as subscribers x monthly price x exchange rate, using a dated exchange rate and net of platform fees.
- Stars (live-stream tips): suited to entertainment, events, music and coaching; same scale conditions.

## 16. Plan-section prompts

- Why Facebook (or Instagram, WhatsApp) for this customer, with dated audience data?
- Which objective, audience tier and ad format will the first campaign use?
- What is the test budget, the target cost per conversation and the stop rule?
- Who replies to enquiries, within what time, and how are leads logged?
- What are the weekly content mix and production routine?
- Which KPIs and review cadence will the plan report?
