# SaaS Funding Request — Stage-by-Stage Reference

The engine's `11-funding-request` skill uses CAMPARI for debt and generic equity structures. SaaS needs a stage-specific funding-request layout. This reference adds it.

## 1. The Stage-Specific Funding Request Pattern

Each stage's funding-request section needs different content. Use the correct template:

### A. Pre-Seed (raising $50k-$500k)

```
THE ASK
  Amount: $___ (in local currency: ___)
  Instrument: SAFE / Convertible Note / Priced Round
  Cap: $___ (post-money equivalent)
  Discount: ___%
  
USE OF FUNDS
  - MVP build (engineering, 6-12 months): ___%
  - Founder + first hire payroll: ___%
  - Customer-development costs (travel, pilots): ___%
  - Legal / incorporation / IP: ___%
  - Buffer: ___%

MILESTONES UNLOCKED
  - PMF signal (Sean Ellis ≥40%, retention curve flat, $10k+ MRR)
  - 15-30 paying customers
  - Ready for seed round in 12-18 months

INVESTOR PROFILE
  Friends & family, angels, micro-funds, accelerators
```

### B. Seed ($500k-$3M)

```
THE ASK
  Amount: $___ 
  Instrument: Priced Seed Round (preferred)
  Valuation: pre-money $___ / post-money $___
  Pro-rata: 1.5×-2× for major investors
  Board: 1 investor seat + 2 founder seats + 1 independent
  Liquidation preference: 1× non-participating
  Anti-dilution: weighted-average broad-based

USE OF FUNDS
  - Engineering team build-out (3-5 hires): ___%
  - First sales hire(s) + sales tools: ___%
  - Marketing channel investment (proving repeatable channel): ___%
  - Customer success / onboarding investment: ___%
  - Infrastructure / compliance (SOC2 roadmap): ___%
  - Working capital buffer: ___%

MILESTONES UNLOCKED
  - Repeatable acquisition channel proven (CAC payback <18 months)
  - $500k-$1.5M ARR
  - LTV:CAC ≥3
  - NRR ≥100%
  - Team grown to 10-15
  - Ready for Series A in 12-24 months

INVESTOR PROFILE
  Seed VCs (Africa: TLcom Stage 1, Partech, P1, Norrsken, Catalyst Fund, Future Africa)
```

### C. Series A ($3M-$15M)

```
THE ASK
  Amount: $___
  Instrument: Priced Series A
  Valuation: pre-money $___ / post-money $___
  Board composition: 2 investor + 2 founder + 1 independent
  Liquidation preference: 1× non-participating
  Protective provisions: standard

USE OF FUNDS
  - Sales team expansion (3-8 AEs + SDRs + sales manager): ___%
  - Marketing scale-up (proven channels): ___%
  - Customer success build-out (CSM + onboarding ops): ___%
  - Product expansion (modules, vertical depth): ___%
  - Engineering / R&D scale (10-25 engineers): ___%
  - Infrastructure / compliance (SOC2, security): ___%
  - G&A build-out (CFO, ops, HR): ___%
  - Geographic expansion (Africa: regional EAC/ECOWAS): ___%

MILESTONES UNLOCKED
  - $3-10M ARR
  - 2-3 sustained acquisition channels
  - Multiple-segment penetration
  - Rule of 40 ≥30
  - NRR ≥110%
  - Team grown to 25-50
  - Ready for Series B in 18-30 months

INVESTOR PROFILE
  Series A VCs (Africa: TLcom main, Partech Africa, Norrsken22, Novastar, 4DX) + DFIs (IFC VC, FMO Ventures, Norfund, BII)
```

### D. Series B / Growth ($15M+)

```
THE ASK
  Amount: $___
  Instrument: Priced Series B / Growth Equity
  Valuation: pre-money $___ / post-money $___
  
USE OF FUNDS
  - International / regional expansion: ___%
  - New product line / vertical: ___%
  - Sales scale-up (multi-segment / multi-region): ___%
  - Operating leverage investments (Rule of 40 path): ___%
  - M&A capacity: ___%

MILESTONES UNLOCKED
  - $20M+ ARR
  - Multi-region operation
  - Multi-product capability
  - Profitability path clear
  - Ready for Series C / strategic / IPO
```

### E. DFI Growth Equity (often $5-50M; long horizon)

```
THE ASK
  Amount: $___
  Instrument: Priced equity (typically minority stake)
  
DFI-SPECIFIC ADDITIONS
  - ESG / impact targets (jobs, beneficiaries, women/youth, climate)
  - IFC Performance Standards compliance plan
  - Local-currency option (some DFIs)
  - Reporting cadence to DFI MIS

USE OF FUNDS
  Same as Series A/B equivalent, plus:
  - ESMS implementation
  - Compliance / certifications
  - Local-content investment
  - Capacity-building investment

MILESTONES UNLOCKED
  Both commercial (ARR, NRR, etc.) and impact (jobs, beneficiaries) milestones
```

## 2. The Use-of-Funds Discipline (Haines-aligned)

For each use-of-funds bucket, show:
- **Incremental cost** (what does it cost; is it capex or opex)
- **Incremental benefit** (what does it produce — quantified)
- **Timing** (when does the cost hit; when does the benefit appear)
- **Downside** (what if this bucket underdelivers)

This is the "business case for the funding ask itself" — fundable plans show this; un-fundable plans show only a pie chart.

## 3. The Milestone Discipline

Use-of-funds should produce specific, measurable milestones, not vague aspirations.

| Bucket | Bad milestone | Good milestone |
|---|---|---|
| Sales hires | "Grow sales" | "3 AEs ramped to $400k quota each by month 9" |
| Marketing | "Increase brand" | "SEO traffic from 5k to 25k/month; 200 organic signups/month by month 12" |
| Product | "Launch features" | "Module 3 launched and used by 30% of customers by month 9" |
| Compliance | "Get certified" | "SOC2 Type 1 achieved by month 6; Type 2 by month 18" |
| Geographic | "Expand to Kenya" | "Kenya entity registered, 5 customers signed, 1 country lead hired by month 9" |

## 4. Living-Plan Discipline for Use of Funds

Post-round, the use of funds becomes the plan. Track:
- Spend per bucket vs plan
- Milestone delivery per bucket
- Variance threshold per bucket
- Quarterly use-of-funds review at board

If a bucket underdelivers, the board variance protocol fires — re-allocate or accept the slip.

## 5. Africa / Uganda Application Notes

- **Use-of-funds in local currency + USD parallel** — DFIs and local banks want local; international investors want USD.
- **DFI ESG / impact use-of-funds** explicitly carved out — ESMS, Performance Standards compliance, capacity-building, local-content investment.
- **Tax-efficient structuring** — most cross-border SaaS rounds use Mauritius / Cayman / Delaware holding companies; structure influences use-of-funds receipt and deployment.
- **Convertible / SAFE** common pre-seed in Africa; price-round preferred for seed and above.
- **Revenue-based finance** is an emerging alternative for SaaS with predictable ARR — no equity dilution; revenue-share for fixed period.
- **Grants stack** with equity — Tony Elumelu, GSMA Innovation Fund, climate / impact grants supplement use of funds.
- **Diaspora / family-office** capital often less structured but more patient; specify governance norms in plan.
- **Local family-office investors** sometimes require board observer; design with this in mind.
