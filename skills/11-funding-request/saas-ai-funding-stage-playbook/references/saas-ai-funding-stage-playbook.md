# SaaS AI Funding Stage Playbook

## 1. The stage ladder with AI-specific signals

| Stage | Generic SaaS signals | AI-specific signals |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-seed** | $0-$50k ARR; founder + tiny team; problem validated | AI prototype in production; ≥1 paying customer with AI feature usage; eval suite started; cost-per-tenant directional |
| **Seed** | $50k-$500k ARR; product-market-fit signals; 10-30 customers | 10-30 AI-paying customers; AI GM >50%; eval coverage >40%; AI-cost-as-%-of-ARR <15%; governance policy drafted |
| **Series A** | $1-$3M ARR or $3-$5M with AI-driven net new; Rule-of-40 ≥30; growth >100% YoY | AI GM >65%; eval coverage >60%; cost engineering visible; moat thesis testable; AI committee operating |
| **Series B** | $5-$15M ARR; Rule-of-40 ≥30; NRR ≥110% | AI GM >70%; eval coverage >75%; vendor concentration <70%; AI policy + committee operating; first proprietary-data moat evidence |
| **Series C / Growth** | $20M+ ARR; Rule-of-40 ≥40; NRR ≥115% | AI GM >75%; eval coverage >85%; external audit / ISO 42001 path; multi-region; enterprise AI compliance |

## 3. Use-of-proceeds AI line items

A round's use-of-proceeds should include specific AI lines, not just "team + product + sales":

| Line | Typical % of round | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| AI infrastructure | 5-15% | Foundation-model APIs, vector DB, eval, observability |
| AI hiring | 15-30% | ML engineers, AI PM, eval lead, governance lead |
| Training-data acquisition | 0-15% | Datasets, partnerships, curation, labelling |
| Eval pipeline build-out | 2-8% | Eval infra + domain expert curators |
| AI governance / compliance | 1-5% | Committee operating, audit, regulatory engagement |
| AI sales / marketing | 5-15% | Sales positioning AI; demos; case studies |

## 4. Round-fund milestones in AI terms

What the round funds should be stated in AI-specific terms:

| Milestone type | Example |
|---|---|
| Eval coverage | "Reach eval coverage 75% by Q4 next year" |
| AI revenue | "Reach $2.5M AI-attributable ARR by month 18" |
| AI GM | "Lift AI GM from 65% to 72% by year-end via cache + model-mix + distillation" |
| AI moat evidence | "Deploy fine-tuned Luganda model with eval beating GPT-4 on dairy-domain benchmark by month 12" |
| AI governance maturity | "AI committee operating + AI policy v2 published + first external bias audit complete by month 9" |
| Regulatory posture | "Achieve NITA-U AI guidelines compliance + EU AI Act limited-risk classification by month 6" |
| Customer milestone | "Land 3 sovereign-AI tenders by month 18" |

## 5. Foundation-model platform-risk response (the most-asked question)

Best-practice answer (template):

> "Foundation-model platform risk is mitigated through (1) multi-provider router operating today with Claude / OpenAI / Cohere / self-hosted Mistral; (2) vertical-depth moat in [vertical] that foundation-model providers will not serve directly; (3) workflow integration moat in [workflow] requiring 18+ months for any competitor to replicate; (4) local-language / data-residency moat that foundation models cannot legally or culturally provide. We track the foundation-model providers' product trajectories monthly and have a 90-day migration playbook tested against [previous model deprecation]."

## 6. Grant + commercial blend (for Africa-context plans)

Common blend:
- **AI-for-good grant** ($100k-$1M) funds training-data acquisition + eval pipeline build + ethics / governance infrastructure + local-language coverage + capacity building
- **Commercial seed / A** ($1-$5M) funds product + GTM + scaling + Series A milestones

This blend dilutes equity less and signals impact-credibility to DFI / strategic investors.

## 9. Africa specifics

- Blended-finance pathway (commercial + AI-for-good + DFI) is often optimal
- DFI / grant cycles are 6-18 months; plan runway accordingly
- Sovereign-AI tender pre-funding is emerging (RW, KE, NG, ZA, EG)
- Diaspora capital + African-roots VCs increasingly AI-comfortable
- USD-denominated funding plus local-currency revenue creates FX management need; reserve / hedge / corridor pricing required
- Public-sector / NGO customer concentration affects round-storytelling
