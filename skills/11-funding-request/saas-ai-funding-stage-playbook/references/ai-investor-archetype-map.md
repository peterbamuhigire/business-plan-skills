# AI Investor Archetype Map — Compact Reference

The full investor archetype detail (including named partners, typical check sizes, thesis notes per fund) lives in `saas-ai-funding-stage-playbook.md`. This file is the compact one-page archetype map for quick targeting.

## Six archetypes

| Archetype | Geography | Stages | What they weight most | What they discount |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AI-specialist VC** | US-led (selective Africa) | Pre-seed → Growth | AI-native architecture; data + workflow moat; eval discipline; technical-founder pedigree | Commodity AI features; weak technical depth |
| **Generalist SaaS VC** | US/EU/global | Seed → Growth | SaaS unit economics (LTV:CAC, NRR, Rule of 40); AI as defensible add-on | Pure AI thesis without SaaS economics |
| **Sovereign-AI fund** | UAE / France / EU / KSA / regional | Seed → Growth | National-strategy alignment; sovereign deployability; ethics + governance; capacity-building | Pure-commercial play with no national-interest angle |
| **DFI** (IFC, AfDB, BII, FMO, Proparco, Norfund, Swedfund, KfW DEG) | Africa / EM | Seed → Growth | Commercial returns + impact KPIs + ESG + IFC PS alignment + gender disagg | Pure-commercial play with no impact dimension |
| **AI-for-good grantmaker** (Mozilla, GSMA, IDRC, Lacuna, Google.org, Microsoft, McGovern, Gates, Wellcome) | Global / Africa | Pre-seed → growth (grant) | Theory-of-change; training-data provenance; community benefit; ethics; sustainability; SDG alignment | Pure-commercial; missing M&E rigour |
| **Strategic acquirer** (Foundation-model providers, incumbent SaaS, vertical incumbents, regional telcos / fintechs, PE rollup) | Various | Series B → exit | Distribution; customer base; team; vertical depth; AI capability vs internal build | Founder-dependency; high burn |

## Stage-thresholds for each archetype

| Archetype | Pre-seed entry | Seed entry | A entry | B entry |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI-specialist VC | $0 ARR with eval running | $200k-$1M AI-ARR | $2-$5M AI-ARR | $7-$15M AI-ARR |
| Generalist SaaS VC | rare | $500k-$1M ARR | $3-$5M ARR | $10-$20M ARR |
| Sovereign-AI fund | rare | $500k-$1M ARR + sovereign-strategy fit | $2-$5M ARR | $5-$15M ARR |
| DFI | rare | rare | $2-$5M ARR + impact baseline | $5-$15M ARR + measured impact |
| AI-for-good grant | from idea to grant pilot | pilot to scale | scaled impact | sustainability + growth |
| Strategic acquirer | rarely buys | rare | rare | $10M+ ARR typical |

## Targeting discipline

For each round:
1. Declare the AI archetype of your company (per `ai-on-saas-business-plan-audit-2026.md` Part 1)
2. Map to 2-4 best-fit investor archetypes
3. Build the pipeline within those archetypes
4. Tailor the pitch to the archetype (the AI-specialist deck differs from the DFI deck)
5. Apply the corresponding bankability weighting (per `saas-ai-bankability-checklist.md` Section 3)

## Common mistakes

- Targeting generalist SaaS VCs with AI-native pitch → discounted
- Targeting AI-specialist VCs with weak technical depth → rejected on diligence
- Targeting DFIs without impact baseline + M&E plan → slow no
- Targeting AI-for-good grantmakers with pure-commercial thesis → rejected on rubric
- Same deck for all archetypes → suboptimal allocation of attention
