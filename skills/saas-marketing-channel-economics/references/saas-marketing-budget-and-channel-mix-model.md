## 2. SaaS Channel Categories with African Context

|---|---|---|

## 3. Channel Scale Ceiling (the saturation reality)

Every channel has a point at which more spend doesn't produce more customers (or produces them at degraded CAC):

| Channel | Typical scale ceiling (US benchmark) |
|---|---|
| Google Ads | $50-500k/month before CAC degrades |
| Facebook Ads | $30-300k/month |
| LinkedIn Ads | $50-200k/month |
| SEO / content | Effectively unlimited; capped by content production capacity |
| Outbound BDR | Capped by BDR team size × productivity |
| Community / referrals | Capped by user base size and product virality |
| Events | Capped by audience size × frequency |

African ceilings are typically 50-80% lower because of smaller addressable digital audiences. But local content / community can scale further as the African internet user base grows.

## 4. The Marketing Arbitrage Concept (Cotton)

The arbitrage: a channel where current market CAC is **below** the LTV-justified ceiling.

```
Maximum CAC tolerable = LTV × max-allowable-CAC-ratio = LTV / 3
Marketing arbitrage exists when channel CAC < (LTV / 3)
```

Pour budget into arbitrage channels until:
- CAC degrades to the LTV ceiling, OR
- The scale ceiling is hit, OR
- A competitor enters the arbitrage and bids it away

**Africa arbitrage examples (2024-26 baseline):**
- Local-language SEO content (no competition for vertical keywords)
- WhatsApp Business automation (massive reach, near-zero marginal cost)
- Sector-specific community / WhatsApp groups (cheap relationship-based acquisition)
- Local PR / sector-media (most companies don't pitch)
- Partner channels with NGOs / DFIs / extension officers (referral-style CAC)

## 6. The Brand vs Performance Split (Cotton)

- 10-25% of marketing spend should go to **brand** (long-term reputation, organic, content, community)
- 75-90% goes to **performance** (measurable CAC-producing activities)

Brand investment is unmeasurable in the short term but compounds. Companies that under-invest in brand find their performance CAC creeping up year after year because the brand isn't carrying weight.

## 8. Africa / Uganda Application Notes

- **Channel concentration is more dangerous** in African SaaS because individual channels have lower scale ceilings — diversification matters more.
- **WhatsApp Business** should be in every African SaaS marketing mix; budget WhatsApp automation tools (Wati, AiSensy, Vonage, 360dialog).
- **Radio + sector media** is the under-utilised "performance" channel for SMB / rural targets.
- **In-person events / expos** disproportionately effective — annual marketing calendar should include 4-8 events.
- **NGO / DFI / public-sector partner channels** are often the highest-leverage B2B acquisition source for vertical SaaS.
- **Local-language content** is a multi-year moat (Swahili, Luganda, Yoruba, Hausa SEO is largely uncontested by major SaaS).
- **Diaspora marketing** (targeting African diaspora as influencers / customers) is an under-utilised channel for African SaaS targeting US/EU expansion.
- **Reference budget allocation** (Year 2 African SaaS at $1M ARR):
  - SEO + content: 25%
  - WhatsApp + community: 15%
  - Paid search: 15%
  - Paid social (LinkedIn, Meta): 10%
  - Events / in-person: 15%
  - Outbound BDR: 10%
  - PR / sector media: 5%
  - Experimental: 5%
