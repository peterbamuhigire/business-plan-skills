- Wrapper: -60 to -80% (multiple is 20-40% of SaaS / AI baseline)
- Average agent (real but incomplete): -15 to +10%
- Strong vertical agent: +25 to +75%
- Exceptional agent (top-quartile all dimensions): +75 to +200%
