# CVP, Break-Even, And Working Capital

## Break-Even Types

Show separate break-even views where relevant:

- Operating break-even: contribution covers operating fixed costs.
- Accounting break-even: profit before tax reaches zero after depreciation and finance cost.
- Cash break-even: operating cash inflows cover operating cash outflows and working-capital needs.
- Debt-service break-even: cash flow covers scheduled principal and interest.
- Product-mix break-even: weighted contribution margin based on expected sales mix.

## CVP Inputs

- Selling price per unit.
- Variable cost per unit.
- Contribution margin per unit and percentage.
- Fixed operating costs.
- Sales mix.
- Capacity constraint where relevant.
- Margin of safety.

## Working Capital

Model:

- AR days.
- AP days.
- Inventory days.
- Deferred revenue or customer deposits.
- VAT/input-output timing.
- PAYE/NSSF/LST and other payroll remittance timing.
- WHT or other tax receivables/payables where applicable.

## Cash Conversion Cycle

Cash conversion cycle equals inventory days plus receivable days minus payable days. Use it to explain funding needs, not just profitability.
