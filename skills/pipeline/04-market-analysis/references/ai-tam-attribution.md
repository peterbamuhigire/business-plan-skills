# AI TAM Attribution — Reference

## 1. The problem

Plans inflate AI TAM by counting all vertical SaaS spend as AI-addressable. Sophisticated investors discount aggressively. This file installs the discipline to honestly attribute AI-related share of TAM / SAM / SOM.

## 2. The two-step discipline

**Step 1:** Compute the standard TAM / SAM / SOM per `04-market-analysis`.

**Step 2:** Apply AI-attribution % per segment. AI-attribution % is the fraction of customer willingness-to-pay that AI specifically drives, not the share of customer that "could use AI."

## 3. AI-attribution rubric (full table)

| Vertical / use case | AI-attribution % | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| AI coding assistants (Copilot, Cursor, Codeium-class) | 80-100% | The product IS AI |
| AI customer-service automation (Intercom Fin, Forethought-class) | 50-80% | AI replaces or augments human; customer pays for automation outcome |
| AI sales-enablement (Gong AI, Clari AI, Outreach AI) | 25-45% | Customer wants sales platform; AI deepens it |
| AI-augmented CRM (lead scoring, deal prediction) | 15-30% | Buyer wants CRM; AI is bonus feature |
| AI in healthcare imaging / diagnostics | 70-100% | AI is the differentiator; quality + speed |
| AI in agricultural advisory (vertical SaaS) | 30-60% | Buyer wants advisory; AI is the new mechanism |
| AI in legal contract analysis (Harvey, Spellbook, Lawgeex) | 60-80% | AI is the work product |
| AI in payroll / HR (resume screening, performance) | 10-25% | Buyer wants payroll; AI is feature |
| AI in vertical workflow (custom RAG on operational data) | 40-70% | AI is the upgrade mechanism |
| AI in finance / banking (fraud, AML, lending decisions) | 30-70% | Depends on whether AI is the primary decision or augments |
| AI in education (Khanmigo-class, adaptive learning) | 40-70% | AI is the new pedagogy |
| AI in insurance (underwriting, claims) | 30-60% | AI is the new decision layer |
| AI in marketing (content, ads, optimisation) | 20-50% | Buyer wants marketing; AI is feature unless AI-native |
| AI in public-sector service delivery (KE Talanta, NG NITDA initiatives) | 40-80% | AI is the new service mechanism in many cases |
| Pure LLM API resale | 100% AI-revenue / 0% defensible | High attribution, weak business |

## 4. AI-readiness tier segmentation

Within any TAM, segment buyers by AI-readiness:

| Tier | Description | Time to convert | % of TAM (2026) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Early adopter** | Already using AI; budget allocated; comfortable with risk | 0-3 month sales cycle | 5-15% of TAM |
| **Fast-follower** | Aware; pilot-ready; needs evidence | 6-12 months | 15-25% of TAM |
| **Mainstream** | Cautious; needs proof + case studies + peer adoption | 18-36 months | 40-55% of TAM |
| **Laggard** | Resistant; will adopt only when forced by competitive pressure | 36+ months | 15-30% of TAM |

For first-3-year SOM, focus is almost entirely on early-adopter + fast-follower segments. Plans claiming mainstream SOM in Year 1 are usually fantasy.

## 5. Geography overlays

For Africa-targeting plans:

| Lens | Effect on TAM |
|---|---|
| **Sovereign-AI / regulated-vertical** | Expansive: adds public-sector AI procurement TAM (KE Talanta, NG NITDA, ZA Presidential Commission); but with longer cycles + concentration risk |
| **Local-language coverage** | Expansive: opens vertical AI TAM that English-only competitors cannot address (Swahili / Hausa / Yoruba / Amharic / Luganda regulated-vertical procurement) |
| **In-country data residency** | Expansive but constraining: addressable subset (residency-sensitive) where you can win, but excludes residency-flexible deals where you have no edge |
| **AI-readiness lag** | Constraining: African enterprise AI-readiness 12-24 months behind US/EU average; ramp slower than US benchmarks suggest |

## 6. Triangulation methods

Don't rely on one TAM source. Triangulate with:

1. **Bottom-up**: target customer × ACV × addressable segment count × AI-attribution %
2. **Top-down**: analyst SaaS market figure × vertical share × AI-attribution %
3. **Benchmark**: competitor public ARR × AI-attribution % (their disclosed AI revenue or implied)
4. **Win-rate**: pipeline-conversion-adjusted addressable

If methods diverge by >2×, declare it and pick the lower for plan SOM.

## 7. Stress-test the TAM

- What if AI-readiness moves slower (Mainstream tier conversion shifts 12 months out)?
- What if foundation-model commoditises the category (everyone has feature parity)?
- What if regulation slows enterprise AI adoption (EU AI Act high-risk classification)?
- What if a category-defining incumbent enters (Microsoft Copilot for the vertical)?

## 8. Worked example — Ugandan Dairy AI Platform

**Standard TAM:**
- 30,000 dairy cooperatives in East Africa (KE + UG + TZ + RW)
- Average ACV achievable: $1,500 / year
- Standard TAM: $45M

**AI-attribution segmentation:**
- 70% of value is operational / management software (would buy without AI): attribution 0%
- 30% of value is AI-specific (Luganda advisory, anomaly detection, summarisation): attribution 100%
- Or: blended AI-attribution = 30% → AI-TAM = $13.5M

**AI-readiness segmentation:**
- Early-adopter cooperatives (large, urban-adjacent, donor-funded): ~8% of 30,000 = 2,400 coops → $3.6M AI-TAM
- Fast-follower (mid-size, organised, with capable boards): ~18% = 5,400 coops → $8.1M AI-TAM
- Mainstream (smaller, traditional): ~50% = 15,000 coops → $22.5M AI-TAM (post-2028 timeframe)
- Laggard: ~24% = 7,200 coops → $10.8M (probably never addressable on SaaS model)

**3-year SOM (early-adopter + fast-follower only):**
- Realistic capture: 10% of fast-follower + 15% of early-adopter
- = 540 + 360 = 900 coops × $1,500 = $1.35M ARR
- AI-attributable: $1.35M × 30% = $405,000 AI-ARR (year 3 realistic)

**This is the honest TAM exhibit. An investor's pushback on inflated TAM disappears.**

## 9. Anti-patterns

- "AI market is $X trillion by 2030" — meaningless; not addressable
- All SaaS TAM = AI TAM
- AI-readiness uniform across segments
- Single-source TAM
- No competitor benchmark
- 100% AI-attribution claimed for all features
- Mainstream + Laggard tiers counted in 3-year SOM

## 10. Living-plan link

TAM refresh annual; AI-attribution review quarterly; competitor AI-revenue benchmarks quarterly; AI-readiness tier shift annual or on major adoption event; sovereign-AI / regulatory-shift on event.
