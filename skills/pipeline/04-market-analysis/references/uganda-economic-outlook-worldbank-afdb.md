| Country context | Policy frameworks | Section 8 |

Decision rules:
1. Base revenue and cost projections on the FY25 actuals and FY26 projections, and treat FY27 growth of 9.8% (oil boost) as an upside scenario only, not the base case.
2. Use the conservative planning FX rate (UGX 3,700/USD) even though the shilling has appreciated (Section 3.3).
3. Use about 18.5% as the commercial lending-rate reference and about 15% for 364-day paper as the risk-free cost-of-funds reference (verify at the time of use).
4. Run the stress cases in Section 7 on any plan with FX, commodity, fuel or ODA exposure.

## 4. Sector Data for Market Analysis

### 4.1 Agriculture

Headline: 24% of GDP, 35% of exports, 68% of the labour force. Arable land 80% of the country (193,200 km2), only 35% cultivated.

Productivity gaps (Source: UEU-26 unless stated, verify):

| Metric | Uganda | Kenya | Tanzania | Rwanda | SSA average |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fertiliser use (kg/ha) | 3-8 | 55 | 20 | 32 | 60 |
| Irrigation (% of potential) | Below 1% | 16% | 10% | n/a | n/a |
| Improved seed use | 4% of crop farmers | n/a | n/a | n/a | n/a |
| Mechanisation | Below 2% of landholdings | 30% | n/a | n/a | n/a |
| Extension service access | 24% of farmers | 75% | n/a | 65% | n/a |
| Digital advice access | 63% | 92% | n/a | 78% | n/a |

Other indicators: land degradation 41% (39% prone to soil erosion); 45,159 registered cooperatives (60% SACCOs, 30% agricultural commodity); agriculture 11.3% of bank lending; agricultural insurance penetration below 1%; irrigation uses below 1% of 3.03 million ha potential; extension reaches only 24% of farmers (benchmarks Kenya 75%, Rwanda 65%).

Agro-processing: about 65% of manufacturing output; about US$13.24 billion a year; about 400,000 workers; animal feed market growing 5% a year (Uganda exported 219 million kg earning US$72 million in 2023); livestock disease losses US$1.1 billion a year. NDP IV agro-industrialisation target: 10.13% agricultural output growth by 2030.

### 4.2 Manufacturing, services and ICT

Industry 26.5% of GDP; construction +12.2% (FY25); agro-processing about 65% of manufacturing output; services 45% of GDP (H1 FY22/23 growth 11.7%); ICT is the fastest-growing services sub-sector; accommodation and food services +10.6% (FY25).

### 4.4 Oil and gas

Tilenga: 190,000 bpd peak (TotalEnergies-led). Kingfisher: 40,000 bpd (CNOOC-led). EACOP: 1,443 km to Tanga, Tanzania, 62-75% complete. Investment above US$11 billion. First oil July 2026; exports start Q1 FY26/27 (verify current status). This drives the FY27 growth surge to 9.8% (UEU-26).

### 4.5 Infrastructure and connectivity

Road density 662 km per 1,000 km2 versus SSA average 1,370 and low-income-country average 2,110; only 0.28% of district roads paved; transport costs are 56% of agricultural costs; 60% of the national road network is vulnerable to flooding (UEU-26). Road density needs to approach the SSA average.

## 7. Risks and Stress-Test Reference Data

### 7.1 Historical shocks

| Period | Shock | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| FY19/20-FY20/21 | COVID-19 | Growth fell; tourism from 5.9% to 2.7% of GDP |
| FY22/23 | Global inflation shock | Inflation peaked at 8.8%; food inflation 22.9% |
| FY22-FY23 | BoU rate cycle | CBR raised 350 bps in 12 months |

### 7.2 Climate (UEU-26 projections)

By the 2040s under current trajectories: total crop production could fall 1.4-2.6%; livestock productivity could fall 3%; 60% of national roads are vulnerable to flooding. Heat stress and water deficit are projected to reduce yields of major staples, with crop-by-crop effects in Table 7 of UEU-26 (consult the source for the table).

### 7.3 External shock vulnerabilities

- Oil price: a current account deficit of 6-8% of GDP creates vulnerability; EACOP delays would extend oil-import dependency.
- ODA: above US$3.1 billion (2020), 6% of GNI, 18% of the national budget; the US supplies more than 40% of Africa's ODA, a concentration risk.
- Commodities: coffee nearly doubled in FY25; a reversal would hit exports and the current account.
- Exchange rate: the shilling appreciated 3% in FY25 and a further 5.6% by October FY26; the UGX 3,700/USD planning rate provides a depreciation buffer.
- Remittances: global remittances to Africa contracted 6.2% in 2023 (AEO-25).

### 7.4 Debt stress indicators

Debt service to exports 42.3% (FY21/22) to 63% (FY22/23) (UEU-21); interest at 28.1% of tax revenue in FY25 versus 24.5% average before; domestic arrears UGX 7.55 trillion (2022); public debt 46.6% (FY24), 51.3% (FY25), 52.8% (FY26 proj), below the Africa median of 65.5%.

### 7.5 Sector resilience reference points

Food insecurity peaked at 56% (Oct/Nov 2022) and recovered to 38% in about two months; tourism can lose more than 50% of its value rapidly.
