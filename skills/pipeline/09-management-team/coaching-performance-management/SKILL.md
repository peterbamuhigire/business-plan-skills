# Coaching Performance Management

## Overview

Use this skill to make the people side of a business plan operational. It turns team claims into role accountability, coaching cadence, mentoring support, performance diagnosis, and capability-gap closure.

## Use When

- Drafting or reviewing management-team, operations, implementation, or risk sections.
- A plan depends on new skills, AI adoption, sales discipline, service standards, or delegated execution.
- The team has capability gaps that must be managed credibly.
- A funder or investor needs confidence that staff performance will be monitored and improved.

## Do Not Use When

- The task only needs short founder biographies.
- The plan has no staffing or execution-capability issue.
- Legal HR policy drafting is required; this skill gives planning guidance, not legal advice.

## Required Inputs

- Current team, roles, reporting lines, gaps, hiring plan, and training budget.
- KPIs or standards each critical role must meet.
- Founder involvement and key-person risks.
- Implementation timeline and operational changes that require adoption.

## Workflow

1. Identify critical roles and the result each role owns.
2. Diagnose each gap as clarity, capability, capacity, motivation, feedback, or system design.
3. Choose coaching, mentoring, managing, hiring, outsourcing, or process redesign as the response.
4. Define cadence: weekly check-ins, monthly KPI reviews, quarterly development reviews.
5. Link training/advisory costs to the financial plan.
6. Add risks and mitigations for unresolved capability gaps.

## Quality Bar

- Every critical role has clear accountability.
- Capability gaps are visible and responsibly handled.
- Training, coaching, and advisory support are budgeted where necessary.
- The plan shows how performance will be reviewed after launch.

## Anti-Patterns

- Hiding key-person dependency.
- Treating advisors as operators.
- Adding staff without onboarding or performance cadence.
- Claiming transformation without training and behaviour-change support.

## Outputs

- Coaching and performance-management plan.
- Management-team capability review.
- Role accountability and KPI table.
- Training/advisory budget assumptions.
- Capability risk notes for the business plan.
