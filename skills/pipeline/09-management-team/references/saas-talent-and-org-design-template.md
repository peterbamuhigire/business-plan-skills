## 3. Stage-Fit Hiring Discipline (Cotton)

The wrong VP can sink a company. The right hire matches the stage, not the pedigree:

| Stage | VP Sales profile | Anti-pattern |
|---|---|---|
| $0-$1M ARR | Player-coach who can sell + hire 2-3 reps | Fortune-500 VP who manages a 50-person team |
| $1-3M ARR | Operator who has built 5-15 person teams | Strategist without bag-carrying recent experience |
| $3-10M ARR | Builder of 15-50 person teams with playbook | Manager from $100M+ ARR company |
| $10-30M ARR | Scaler with $10-30M experience | First-time-VP without scale-up reps |
| $30M+ | Multi-region / multi-segment operator | Specialist in one segment only |

## 4. The Founder-to-Hire Transition

Founder roles evolve:
- **Founder-led** ($0-$1M): founder is CEO + Head of Product + Head of Sales + recruiter
- **Hire-supported** ($1-3M): first executive hires (Head of GTM; VP Eng) — founder still in everything
- **Executive team** ($3-10M): C-suite formed; founder transitions to vision + strategy + investor + key customer
- **Operating team** ($10M+): full leadership team operates; founder is CEO of the company, not the operations

Most founder failure modes happen at the $3-10M transition. Plan for it explicitly.

## 5. Hiring Sequence Triggers

Don't hire by calendar; hire by trigger:

| Hire | Trigger |
|---|---|
| First AE | Founder is closing >$300k ARR / year and bottleneck |
| Sales Manager | 3+ AEs; founder can't coach them all |
| Head of GTM / VP Sales | $1-3M ARR with multi-rep team |
| First CSM | First 20+ paying customers; renewal cycle starting |
| Head of CS | 5+ CSMs OR $3M ARR with retention focus |
| VP Engineering | 5+ engineers; CTO bottleneck |
| Head of Marketing | $1-3M ARR with multiple channels |
| CFO | Series A approaching OR $5M ARR OR financial complexity |
| Head of People | $3-5M ARR; 25-50 person team |
| COO | $10M+ ARR with operational complexity |

## 6. Compensation Philosophy

- **Founder salary**: minimal at pre-PMF; market-rate from $1M ARR
- **Equity**: ESOP 10-15% post-money typical at seed; refresh at each round
- **Senior hires**: equity-heavy for early hires; cash-heavy as company matures
- **Sales comp**: 50/50 base/variable for AE; 70/30 for SDR; 60/40 for managers
- **CS comp**: 70/30 or 80/20 base/variable
- **Engineering comp**: 90/10 typically, or 100% base with equity
- **Geographic adjustment**: scale by city / country COL; track tightly

## 8. Africa / Uganda Application Notes

- **Senior-talent depth is shallow** in African ICT — plan for executive search retainers, international recruiting, executive coaching, secondment programmes.
- **Compensation**: scale OTE / salary down 50-70% vs US benchmarks for local hires; equity becomes more meaningful relative to cash.
- **Diaspora hires** (Africans returning) are often the highest-leverage senior hires — combine African context with international-company experience.
- **Distributed model** is increasingly viable — Nigerian engineers, Kenyan product, South African design, Egyptian customer-success.
- **Local-content / nationality requirements** sometimes apply (especially public-sector engagement) — plan accordingly.
- **Founder transition** is often harder in African contexts because of family-business norms; plan board governance early.
- **Talent retention**: investment in training (e.g., LinkedIn Learning, A Cloud Guru) and structured career paths matter more than perks.
- **Local-language fluency** in customer-facing roles is a hiring filter, not a nice-to-have.
