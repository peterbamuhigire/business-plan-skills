# Coaching And Performance Management

Self-contained synthesis prepared from supplied coaching, mentoring, and managing source material. Use it to strengthen management-team, execution, and operating-model sections.

## Management Capability Lens

A credible management section should show not only who is on the team, but how performance will be guided, corrected, and developed.

| Mode | Use when | Output in plan |
|---|---|---|
| Coaching | Person can perform with feedback and practice | Manager cadence, KPIs, check-ins |
| Mentoring | Person needs judgement, career growth, or domain exposure | Advisor/mentor role and learning goals |
| Managing | Role, standard, accountability, or performance gap is unclear | Job scorecard, supervision, corrective process |

## Performance Problem Diagnosis

Before adding headcount, diagnose clarity, capability, capacity, motivation, feedback, and system design. A staffing problem may actually be a process, tools, incentives, or management-cadence problem.

## Coaching Cadence

For key roles, define weekly operational check-in, monthly KPI review, quarterly development review, documented action commitments, escalation path for persistent gaps, and training or mentoring support.

## Business-Plan Uses

- Management team: show leadership development and supervision maturity.
- Operations plan: show how standards are maintained.
- Implementation timeline: include training and adoption phases.
- Risk analysis: treat key-person and capability gaps as managed risks.
- Funding request: justify training, advisory, or management-system costs.

## Red Flags

- Team bios with no accountability.
- Hiring plan with no onboarding or performance system.
- Founder-dependent execution with no delegation path.
- Training budget absent despite major process or AI transformation.
- Advisors listed without meeting cadence or decision role.
