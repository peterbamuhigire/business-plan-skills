# Facility-Change Continuity and Cutover Reference

Use for relocations, operating-site changes, ERP/data migrations, service transitions, and other complex changes where continuity matters. Synthesized from *Facility Move Playbook*; validate site, safety, regulatory, contractual, and technical requirements independently.

## Gates

1. **Charter and decision rights:** define scope, sponsor, workstream owners, non-negotiables, affected services, budget boundary, and escalation route.
2. **Current-state baseline:** inventory people, assets, systems, dependencies, service volumes, capacity, regulatory obligations, risks, and critical failure modes.
3. **Readiness:** confirm future-state design, procurement, access, safety, data, communications, training, contingency capacity, acceptance criteria, and rollback owner.
4. **Continuity plan:** protect critical services with sequencing, buffers, manual fallback, communication, data backup, reconciliation, and incident escalation.
5. **Cutover decision:** require evidence for prerequisites, go/no-go authority, timed runbook, dry run, contact tree, rollback trigger, and first-day monitoring.
6. **Stabilisation:** track incidents, backlog, service level, quality, safety, user/beneficiary feedback, cost variance, and unresolved exceptions until the agreed exit threshold is met.
7. **Closeout and learning:** reconcile assets, costs, records, approvals, and open risks; capture lessons, update standards/runbooks/training, assign residual risks, and schedule re-audit.

## Decision rules

- Do not declare ready because activities are complete; require evidence against each prerequisite.
- Do not cut over while a critical service has no tested fallback or rollback path.
- Do not close the project while continuity, safety, financial, data, or acceptance exceptions lack an owner and due date.
- Standardise only after stabilisation evidence and owner sign-off.
