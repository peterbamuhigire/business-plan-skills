# SaaS ARR-Milestone Implementation Timeline

SaaS planning by calendar dates (Q1 Y1 / Q2 Y1) misses the structural reality: everything breaks at certain ARR / headcount milestones. This reference re-bases the implementation timeline on **ARR milestones**, not calendar dates, with calendar mapping as secondary.

## 1. The Master ARR-Milestone Map

```
                    What changes at this milestone
$0 (pre-PMF)        Founder is the operating system; no playbook yet
                    
$100k ARR           First non-founder sale; first customer-success process needed
                    
$500k ARR           First sales manager; first marketing-channel proven; first CSM
                    
$1M ARR             Rule-of-3 broken; need org redesign; VP Sales feasible
                    
$3M ARR             Rule-of-10 breaks; need second layer of management
                    Pods emerge; head of GTM; CFO hire
                    
$10M ARR            Rule-of-30 breaks; full executive team; multi-channel
                    Multiple sales pods; full CS function; SOC2 mandatory
                    
$30M ARR            Rule-of-100 breaks; regions; verticals; segments
                    
$100M ARR           Public-company-like discipline; multi-region; pre-IPO if applicable
```

(Per Cotton: 3, 10, 30, 100, 300, 1000 are the headcount milestones; corresponding ARR milestones depend on revenue-per-employee, which for healthy SaaS is $200-500k.)

## 2. The Living Timeline (calendar mapped to ARR)

For Section 13 (Implementation Timeline), produce both views:

### View A: Calendar (Gantt-style)
- 90-day sprint cycles
- Quarterly milestones (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4 across Y1, Y2, Y3)
- Specific feature launches, hires, certifications

### View B: ARR-Milestone (event-based)
- Triggered events: when ARR hits X, do Y
- Not date-driven but reality-driven
- Includes if/then branches

The plan should commit to View A for Year 1 (calendar-bound milestones); View B for Years 2-5 (ARR-bound triggers).

## 3. Worked Example — Ugandan Vertical SaaS Timeline

**View A (Year 1 calendar):**
```
Q1 (Jan-Mar)
  - Hire 1st AE (founder transitions sales handoff)
  - Launch Module 1 (basic milk recording)
  - 5 paying customers; UGX 25M ARR
  - Customer-conversation cadence formalised
  - Pricing experiment 1

Q2 (Apr-Jun)
  - Onboarding pipeline formalised (90-day onboarding milestones)
  - 12 paying customers; UGX 60M ARR
  - Module 2 launch (payment automation)
  - First field-officer training session
  - Pricing experiment 2

Q3 (Jul-Sep)
  - Channel partnership with MAAIF formalised
  - 22 paying customers; UGX 110M ARR
  - Hire 2nd AE + 1st CSM
  - SOC2 Type 1 roadmap kickoff
  - Pricing experiment 3

Q4 (Oct-Dec)
  - 35 paying customers; UGX 200M ARR
  - Year 1 close: review against Sean Ellis test; cohort retention review
  - Plan year 2 with refresh; new MSPOT
```

**View B (Years 2-3 ARR-triggered):**

```
TRIGGER: ARR hits UGX 250M (~$67k)
ACTIONS:
  - Hire 3rd AE
  - Hire 1st marketing-content lead
  - Pricing increase 8% for new customers
  - Launch Module 3 (farmer scoring)

TRIGGER: ARR hits UGX 500M (~$135k)
ACTIONS:
  - Hire 4th AE + 2nd SDR + sales manager (player-coach)
  - Hire 2nd CSM
  - Begin Kenya market entry (1 BDR + content)
  - SOC2 Type 1 achieved

TRIGGER: ARR hits UGX 1B (~$270k) — Rule of 3 broken (~15 staff)
ACTIONS:
  - Hire Head of GTM
  - Hire CFO (or fractional)
  - Org redesign — sales / product / CS / G&A separation
  - Series A planning begins

TRIGGER: ARR hits UGX 3B (~$810k) — Rule of 10 broken (~30 staff)
ACTIONS:
  - Hire VP Sales (full-time)
  - Hire VP Engineering
  - Pod structure for sales (SMB pod + Enterprise pod)
  - Geographic expansion: Tanzania + Rwanda entry
  - SOC2 Type 2 achieved
  - Series A close
```

## 4. The "Pre-Phase 0 Regulatory Gate" Pattern (engine standard)

Before launch, the regulatory gate must be cleared:
- Business registration (URSB / CAC / etc.)
- Tax registration (URA / FIRS / KRA)
- Data-protection registration (NITA-U / NDPR / DPC / etc.)
- Sector licence (fintech, healthtech, etc.)
- Bank account
- IP / trademark registration

This is "Phase 0" — clear it before any other activity.

## 6. Africa / Uganda Application Notes

- **Procurement-cycle alignment**: African enterprise / public-sector procurement cycles affect quarterly bookings; align sprint cadence to fiscal year of major customer segments.
- **Donor-cycle alignment**: if donor-funded customers are >20%, align timeline to donor budget cycles.
- **Regulatory milestones** often dominate Phase 0: NITA-U registration (Uganda), KCCA permits, sector licences. Build 8-16 weeks for clearance.
- **Office / physical-presence requirements**: some sector licences require physical office; budget accordingly.
- **Multi-country expansion**: each country's regulatory gate is a separate Phase 0. Don't underestimate the timeline.
- **Tax-year**: Uganda fiscal year is Jul-Jun; Kenya is Jul-Jun; Nigeria is Jan-Dec; SA is Mar-Feb; align with major customer segment.
- **Holiday / cultural windows**: Eid, Christmas/New Year, Lent — affect African business cadence; budget for low-velocity periods.
