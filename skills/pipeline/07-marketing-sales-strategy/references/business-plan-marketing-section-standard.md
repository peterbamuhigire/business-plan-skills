# Business-plan marketing section standard

Parent: [Marketing and Sales Strategy](../SKILL.md).

When to read: every time Section 07 is drafted or reviewed. It defines what
the section must contain, how deep to go for the plan's size and reader, how it
reuses the marketing-plan orchestrator's methods without duplicating them,
and how it reconciles with sections 04, 05, 06 and 10.

## 1. Section 07 or a standalone marketing plan?

| Situation | Use | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A business plan for a lender, investor, DFI, grant funder or board needs to show how the business will win and keep customers | Section 07 (this skill) | The reader needs a credible, reconciled summary of commercial logic, not an operating manual |
| The client needs a document the marketing team will run for 12 months (calendar, media plan, briefs, control table) | `marketing-plan-orchestrator` | Operating depth: channel test cards, creative brief, war-room cadence |
| Both | Write the standalone marketing plan first; Section 07 summarises it and cites it as an appendix | One source of truth; the section cannot drift from the plan |
| A campaign or launch inside an existing plan | `marketing-plan-orchestrator` at campaign scale | Section 07 is not rewritten for every campaign |

## 2. Required content of Section 07

Write each sub-section as decision plus evidence, scaled by the table in
section 3. Methods live in the orchestrator references named in brackets.

1. **Marketing objectives** — two to five SMART objectives calibrated to the
   location and scope, cascaded from the business objective, each with
   baseline, target, date and the source of its line in the sand
   ([SMART objective builder](../../../marketing-sales/marketing-plan-orchestrator/references/smart-objectives-builder.md);
   [location and scope calibration](../../../marketing-sales/marketing-plan-orchestrator/references/location-and-scope-calibration.md)).
2. **Target segments and positioning** — the chosen segments (carried from
   Section 05, which proves the four target-market tests), the positioning
   statement with a checkable reason to believe, and two or three message
   themes ([toolkit](../../../marketing-sales/marketing-plan-orchestrator/references/situation-and-market-toolkit.md)).
3. **Strategy and marketing mix** — the strategic choice (and the rejected
   alternative), push versus pull, and a decision per P (4Ps or 7Ps).
4. **Channel plan** — the channels chosen after considering the full set, the
   reason, and the first tests with spend caps
   ([Bullseye](../../../marketing-sales/marketing-plan-orchestrator/references/traction-channel-bullseye.md)).
5. **Advertising and promotion** (when paid media is material) — objectives,
   triangulated budget with floor, and the media mix in one table
   ([advertising and media plan](../../../marketing-sales/marketing-plan-orchestrator/references/advertising-and-media-plan.md)).
6. **Sales plan** — structure, process, conversion ratios from records or
   tests, capacity; for B2B, grading and contact economics
   ([sales plan](../../../marketing-sales/marketing-plan-orchestrator/references/sales-plan-and-account-coverage.md)).
7. **Retention and customer value** — active-customer definition, CLV, the
   retention mechanism and the affordable acquisition cost
   ([marketing economics](../../../marketing-sales/marketing-plan-orchestrator/references/marketing-economics-and-budget.md)).
8. **Budget and reconciliation** — marketing budget by category and year
   (monthly for year one where the lender's template requires it), equal to the
   P&L marketing line; demand-to-cash bridge feeding Section 10.
9. **KPIs and control** — OMTM, three to five guard-rails, review cadence and
   owners ([KPI and control plan](../../../marketing-sales/marketing-plan-orchestrator/references/kpi-dashboard-and-control-plan.md)).
10. **Assumptions and risks** — the commercial assumptions still untested, the
    test that will resolve each, and the stop rule.

## 3. Depth by plan type

| Plan type | Length of Section 07 | Must include | May summarise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Micro-loan or SACCO application | 1–2 pages | Objectives, customers, how sales are made, budget, three KPIs | Channel choice and media in one short table |
| SME bank or DFI plan | 3–6 pages | All ten items | Advertising detail; control cadence |
| Equity investor plan | 4–8 pages | All ten items with unit economics prominent (CAC, CLV, payback) | Media scheduling |
| Grant proposal | As the funder's template dictates | Beneficiary or customer reach logic, cost per outcome, sustainability of demand | Commercial media detail |
| Internal board plan | 3–6 pages | Objectives, budget reconciliation, control and review | Background analysis already agreed |

## 4. Reconciliation with neighbouring sections

| Item in Section 07 | Must equal or agree with | Owning section |
|---|---|---|
| Segment sizes and served market | Section 04 market ladder and Section 05 segment counts | 04 and 05 |
| Competitive claims and positioning proof | Section 06 grids and advantage | 06 |
| Prices and offer | Section 03 pricing and product | 03 |
| Volumes, conversion, lags, sales headcount and commissions | Section 10 revenue drivers, payroll and cash flow | 10 |
| Marketing budget | Section 10 P&L marketing line and cash flow | 10 (with the finance engine) |
| Launch activities and dates | Section 13 implementation timeline | 13 |
| Marketing and compliance risks | Section 12 risk register | 12 |

A conflict returns to the owning section; Section 07 never silently adopts a
different number.

## 5. Strategy-type emphasis

Read the client's `strategy_type` from intake and apply the matching row of
the strategy-type emphasis table in the
[business-plan phrase bank](../../../language/writing-quality/references/business-plan-phrase-bank.md):
it states which proof a reader will expect in Section 07 (for example, an
efficiency improver must show the saving and how much is passed to customers;
a niche marketer must show why general providers serve the niche poorly).

## 6. Wording

Use the Section 07 entry of the
[phrase bank, sections 01–08](../../../language/writing-quality/references/business-plan-phrase-bank-sections-01-08.md):
keywords, slot-templates, tone rules and before-and-after rewrites. Every
number shows its evidence class ([evidence discipline](../../../marketing-sales/marketing-plan-orchestrator/references/evidence-discipline-for-marketing-claims.md)).

## 7. Section 07 acceptance checks

- Objectives pass the SMART quality test and are calibrated to the place and scope.
- Segments and sizes match sections 04 and 05; competitive claims match Section 06.
- At least one credible alternative strategy or channel is named and rejected with a reason.
- The sales plan shows ratios from records or tests, and capacity.
- The budget equals the P&L marketing line, year by year.
- KPIs have owners, lines and review dates.
- Untested assumptions are listed with their tests and stop rules.
- The section is not called bankable or investor-ready on its own; that judgement belongs to the plan-level gates.
