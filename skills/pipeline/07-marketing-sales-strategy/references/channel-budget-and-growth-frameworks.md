## Pricing Strategy Options

| Strategy | Best For | Main Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Cost-plus | Simple products and low-competition markets | Ignores customer value |
| Value-based | Differentiated offers | Requires strong proof and positioning |
| Competitive | Commodity markets | Margin pressure |
| Penetration | Market share capture | Hard to raise prices later |
| Skimming | Innovation and early adopters | Invites competition |
| Freemium | SaaS and digital products | Conversion-rate risk |
| Subscription | Predictable recurring value | Churn management |
| Tiered | Diverse customer segments | Offer complexity |

## Guerrilla and Small-Business Tactics

Use low-cost tactics when the plan has limited cash but can invest time, relationship-building, or creativity.

Core guerrilla plan:

1. Define the action marketing should cause.
2. State the competitive advantage.
3. Narrow the target audience.
4. Choose the marketing weapons.
5. Define the niche.
6. Set budget as a percentage of projected revenue.
7. Commit to repetition and measurement.

High-impact weapons include alliance marketing, guarantees, education-based marketing, follow-up systems, referral programmes, community building, testimonials, PR, speaking, customer review meetings, and newsletters.

Small-business growth must address three levers:

1. Get new customers through alliances, networking, PR, advertising, events, and direct sales.
2. Increase spend per customer through upsell, cross-sell, packaging, and reviews.
3. Increase purchase frequency through communication calendars, newsletters, loyalty, and offers.

## Budget Framework

| Business Stage | Marketing as % of Revenue | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-revenue or launch | 15-25% of projected revenue | Awareness and first acquisition |
| Early growth | 10-20% of revenue | Acquisition and activation |
| Scaling | 8-15% of revenue | Efficiency, retention, expansion |
| Mature | 5-10% of revenue | Retention, brand, market defence |

Channel budget table fields:

- Channel
- Monthly budget
- Expected CAC
- Expected customers
- ROI target
- Learning objective
