# Agentic AI Business Plan Gates

Self-contained synthesis prepared from supplied agentic AI and AI transformation source material. Use when a business plan includes AI agents, automation, or AI-enabled operating leverage.

## Autonomy Gate

| Level | Plan wording allowed | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Assist | AI drafts or analyses for staff review | Workflow owner and review step |
| Recommend | AI recommends next actions | Baseline metric and approval process |
| Supervised act | AI acts in low-risk bounded cases | Tool scopes, logs, escalation, rollback |
| Conditional autonomy | AI acts under pre-approved conditions | Eval results, kill switch, incident process |
| Managed autonomy | AI owns an operational outcome | Audit cadence, budget caps, proven ROI |

Do not describe autonomous AI as a core efficiency driver unless the plan explains the control model.

## AI Use-Case Scoring

Prioritise AI use cases with measurable pain, clean data, named ownership, stable tool access, reversible actions, and clear customer or cost impact. Defer use cases with unclear ownership, poor data, high-stakes decisions, or no evidence of adoption capacity.

## Business-Plan Evidence Checklist

- Specific workflow being improved.
- Current baseline: time, cost, errors, conversion, response time, or service quality.
- Proposed AI role: assist, recommend, supervised act, or autonomous.
- Human review boundary.
- Data sources and quality risks.
- Tool stack and integration dependency.
- Cost model: subscriptions, implementation, training, monitoring, and failure handling.
- Expected benefit and payback.
- Governance: owner, policy, audit trail, kill switch, and escalation.

## Transformation Rule

AI transformation is mostly people and process. The plan must show training, adoption, workflow redesign, governance, and ownership, not only tools. If AI savings depend on staff behaviour changing, say so explicitly.

## Investor/Lender Red Flags

- "AI will reduce costs" with no baseline labour or error-cost data.
- Autonomous customer communication with no review or escalation.
- AI tool list with no process redesign.
- Data access assumed but not evidenced.
- No owner for model outputs, prompt maintenance, or incidents.
- No budget for training, monitoring, or governance.
