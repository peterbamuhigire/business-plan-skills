# Kaizen Wave — Human English and Reader-Centred Craft

Date: 2026-08-27
Scope: business-plan language, content, proposal, pitch, report, blog, and client-facing message surfaces.

## Baseline

This was a language-craft screening, not a product or bankability certification. The raw screening score was 58/100; the published score remains capped under the engine’s Kaizen rule. Existing strengths were strong anti-slop routing, evidence discipline, and a substantial writing-quality layer. Gaps were fragmented channel guidance, weak separation between grammar and style, no local synthesis of the supplied books, and a few inherited mechanical/OCR-like sentence-count rules.

## Root cause

The engine had good safeguards, but they were distributed across writing, premium, language, and anti-slop skills. A user entering through one route could miss the same reader, genre, grammar, and proof discipline used elsewhere.

## Improvement implemented

- Added `book-extractions/human-english-craft-synthesis-2026.md` with source provenance, limits, five passes, register matrix, grammar/lexical checks, anti-slop tests, and original examples.
- Routed the reference through `AGENTS.md`, `writing-quality`, and `anti-ai-slop`.
- Corrected malformed fixed sentence-count guidance and replaced it with thought-led paragraph guidance.
- Preserved the financial, market-evidence, critical-thinking, and anti-slop gates; the language overlay cannot certify unsupported claims.

## Engine-level experiment and evidence

Experiment: verify that the local reference exists and is linked from the engine router and two anchor writing gates.
Success criterion: all required files and links resolve in a clean repository.
Result: PASS — the local reference and intended router/anchor links resolve. No claim of output-quality improvement is made until representative drafts receive human review.

## Guardrails and rollback

If a validator, routing test, or downstream skill fails, remove only the new reference links and restore the prior route while retaining the source-limit record. Never weaken the evidence or anti-slop gates to make the language change pass.

Two human editors should score reader fit, grammar, specificity, genre, rhythm, and proof at least 4/5, with zero fabricated claims and no blocking anti-slop finding. Re-audit date: 2026-09-26.
