# Hospitality, hotel and restaurant Kaizen - 2026-09-14

## Change

Added a bankable-plan overlay that forces format classification and separate
models for rooms, F&B, events and ancillary revenue. It requires monthly ramp,
working capital, sources and uses, debt service, downside cases, operating
capacity, staffing, permits, food safety, technology, and a dated assumption
register. It treats old guide/book ratios as hypotheses, not local facts.

## Evidence discipline

Books supplied by the user informed durable operating and financial reasoning.
Current market, tax, licensing, rates, wages, demand and regulatory claims must
be verified in country and sector registers. Finance-touching work routes to
Chwezi Accounting Doctrine. Missing lender, client, market or reviewer evidence
remains `NOT_ASSESSED`; “bankable” is not inferred from polished prose.

## Experiment and gate

Hypothesis: revenue-centre separation and assumption traceability reduce model
contradictions. Measure formula/reconciliation defects, unsupported benchmarks,
scenario coverage and reviewer queries on the next plan. Rollback by removing
the overlay link; retain the pre-change pipeline. Next review: 2026-10-14.
