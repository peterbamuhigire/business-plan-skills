# Recommended Books and Reading Material

## Must Encode Now

| Title | Author/issuer | Gap filled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bankable Business Plans | Edward Rogoff | Bank/lender readiness and plan structure. |
| Business Plans That Work | Jeffry Timmons et al. | Investor-facing planning and venture discipline. |
| Investment Valuation | Aswath Damodaran | Valuation, risk, and scenario modelling. |
| Financial Modeling | Simon Benninga | Model structure, formulas, scenarios, and auditability. |
| Business Model Generation | Alexander Osterwalder and Yves Pigneur | Business model logic and customer value architecture. |

## Deepen Later

| Title | Author/issuer | Gap filled |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Lean Startup | Eric Ries | Hypothesis testing and build-measure-learn discipline. |
| Venture Deals | Brad Feld and Jason Mendelson | Fundraising terms and investor implications. |
| Good Strategy Bad Strategy | Richard Rumelt | Strategic diagnosis and coherent action. |
