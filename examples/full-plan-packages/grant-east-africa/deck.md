# Grant-panel deck script

Design: Source Serif 4 headings, IBM Plex Sans body, IBM Plex Mono indicator definitions.

1. **The grant buys a bounded test, not an assumed impact.**
2. **Beneficiaries and selection rules are explicit.**
3. **The baseline is a release gate.**
4. **Activities connect to measurable response behaviour.**
5. **Partners have defined delivery and evidence duties.**
6. **MEL distinguishes output, outcome and inference.**
7. **Safeguarding and data controls precede collection.**
8. **Restricted funds reconcile to approved activities.**
9. **Midline evidence can stop or redesign the pilot.**
10. **Sustainability is tested through operating value and willingness to pay.**
11. **Progress requires eligibility, baseline, budget and control evidence.**

Every slide note points to a plan section, indicator definition, model assumption or claim ID.
