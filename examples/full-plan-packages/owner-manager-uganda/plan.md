# LakeLink Controlled Growth Plan — owner-manager exemplar

> Fictional worked example. It supports an internal decision; it does not imply external funding.

## 1. Decision and executive case

The owner must choose whether to add a second installation team now, stage growth through customer
deposits, or hold capacity until collections improve. The plan favours reversible commitments and
cash survival over presentation scale.

## 2. Current operating position

The exemplar assumes a small installed base and owner-led selling. A real plan starts from bank,
receivables, contract, device, support-ticket and payroll records. Unsupported recollection is
labelled and does not become an opening balance.

## 3. Customer and sales priorities

Focus is on customers with repeatable site needs, acceptable payment behaviour and support fit.
The pipeline is weighted by evidenced stage and next action. A named prospect is not revenue until
the plan's recognition and cash criteria are met.

## 4. Product and service promise

The offer includes installation, alerts, support and reporting with explicit exclusions. Service
levels reflect available staff and connectivity; the plan does not promise national coverage before
capacity and recovery procedures exist.

## 5. Operations and control system

Weekly controls cover device custody, installation acceptance, alert failures, invoices,
collections, bank reconciliation and exceptions. Duties are separated as far as the team permits;
owner overrides are logged and reviewed.

## 6. Financial choices

The overlay compares hire-now, staged-contractor and hold cases. It tests deposits, collection days,
device purchases, payroll commitment and minimum cash. Profit does not substitute for cash, and no
plug forces the balance sheet to tie.

## 7. Regulatory and tax gates

Entity, tax, product/import, ICT/data and e-waste screens are assessed against current competent
sources. Rates and thresholds are not copied from this exemplar. Unresolved treatment is routed to
the finance or professional reviewer before pricing or cash-flow decisions.

## 8. Implementation and weekly cadence

Weeks 1–2 reconcile records and backlog; weeks 3–4 test deposits and contractor capacity; month 2
runs a bounded cohort; month 3 reviews cash, quality and support load. A permanent hire occurs only
after the documented trigger is met.

## 9. Risks and stop rules

Stop new commitments if minimum cash is breached, receivables exceed the agreed range, installations
fail acceptance, device losses are unreconciled or a regulatory stop condition applies. Recovery is
smaller cohorts, deposits, collection action, supplier renegotiation or delayed hiring.

## 10. Owner decision and evidence

The recommended branch is selected only after the formula map, opening balances, cash scenarios and
control evidence pass. The owner-board simulation records the countercase and the date for the next
decision rather than treating the plan as a one-off document.
