# Owner-manager decision deck script

Design: Source Serif 4 headings, IBM Plex Sans body, IBM Plex Mono cash and control exhibits.

1. **The decision is when to add capacity without exhausting cash.**
2. **Opening records must reconcile before growth is modelled.**
3. **Payment behaviour narrows the priority customer list.**
4. **The service promise is bounded by current capacity.**
5. **Three capacity choices expose different commitments.**
6. **Minimum cash, not accounting profit, controls the branch.**
7. **Deposits and collections reduce working-capital pressure.**
8. **Weekly controls make growth observable.**
9. **Regulatory and tax gaps stop affected commitments.**
10. **A dated trigger—not optimism—authorises the permanent hire.**
