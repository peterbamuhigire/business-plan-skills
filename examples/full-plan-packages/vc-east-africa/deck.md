# VC committee deck script

Design: Source Serif 4 headings, IBM Plex Sans body, IBM Plex Mono cohort and unit-economics exhibits.

1. **LakeLink targets a costly operating failure with a measurable workflow.**
2. **Customer evidence—not a regional headline—defines the opportunity.**
3. **Cohorts reveal whether recurring value persists.**
4. **Hardware and subscription economics are separated.**
5. **The acquisition engine is tested by segment and channel.**
6. **Expansion follows operating readiness, not flags on a map.**
7. **Workflow integration and data quality are the moat hypotheses.**
8. **The team plan follows volume triggers.**
9. **The round funds named milestones and runway.**
10. **Downside retention and slower sales expose the next financing need.**
11. **Valuation is a range with dilution and countercase.**
12. **Progress depends on cohort, model, security and gate evidence.**
