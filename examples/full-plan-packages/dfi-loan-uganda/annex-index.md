# DFI annex and acceptance index

| Annex | Required evidence | Acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Signed contracts and site schedule | Counterparty, term, price and termination reconcile to model |
| B | Formula map and finance review | No broken links; scenarios, balance and debt checks evidenced |
| C | Supplier quotations and procurement record | Specification, landed cost, warranty and selection trace agree |
| D | Sector and environmental/social screens | Competent source, date, owner, status and model implication recorded |
| E | Management and controls | Asset register, receivables, covenant and incident owners evidenced |

Release remains blocked while any required annex is absent or marked `not assessed`.
