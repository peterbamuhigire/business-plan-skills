# DFI debt committee deck script

Design: Source Serif 4 headings, IBM Plex Sans body, IBM Plex Mono figures. One claim and its source
state per slide; no decorative market-size graphics.

1. **The decision is staged asset finance, not unrestricted growth capital.** Show instrument, uses,
   milestone and unresolved conditions.
2. **Contracted sites are the repayment engine.** Show signed versus pipeline sites separately.
3. **Monitoring addresses a measured operating loss.** Show customer baseline method; mark absent
   baseline evidence.
4. **Installation and subscription cash events are modelled separately.** Show billing-to-cash bridge.
5. **A gated deployment process limits stranded equipment.** Show survey, acceptance and alert-test gates.
6. **Operating cash—not enterprise value—services debt.** Show base and downside coverage with formula-map status.
7. **Delayed sites and collections are the controlling downside.** Show cash trough and funded response.
8. **Procurement and environmental/social controls precede drawdown.** Show open action-plan items.
9. **Management ownership is explicit.** Show contract, deployment, asset-register and covenant owners.
10. **Progress requires five evidenced conditions.** List contract, model, gate, procurement and E&S acceptance tests.

Speaker notes must cite plan section, workbook cell or evidence-manifest claim ID for every
load-bearing statement. The last slide says “progress to human review,” never “approved.”
