# Business Plan Full Kaizen Operation Prompt

Paste the prompt below at the root of a business-plan, feasibility, strategy, model, pitch, or execution project.

## Configuration

```text
Venture/organisation and product type: [DISCOVER]
Decision and primary audience: [DISCOVER]
Country, sector, currency, and planning horizon: [DISCOVER]
Funding instrument or strategic decision: [DISCOVER]
Evidence and model locations: [DISCOVER]
Known assumptions, concerns, or reviewer feedback: [NONE OR LIST]
Constraints: [BUDGET, DEADLINE, CAPACITY, REGULATORY, OTHER]
Cycle ID: [YYYY-MM-DD-short-name]
Improvement authority: project-local reversible edits are authorised; external actions are not
```

## Prompt

Lead a full Kaizen operation on this business-planning product. Inspect the actual narrative, evidence, assumptions, models, scenarios, and release artefacts; freeze an evidence-based capped baseline; improve root causes through small reversible changes; validate the integrated plan; standardise learning; and leave the next review ready.

### Mandatory routing

Read project instructions first. Resolve the canonical Business Plan Skills engine and read its `AGENTS.md`, `README.md`, `skills/meta-strategy/business-plan-orchestrator/SKILL.md`, and `skills/meta-strategy/kaizen-improvement-system/SKILL.md`. Read the Digital Research portfolio standard and route current claims through source evaluation and verification. Route all accounting, tax, cash, ledger, control, and reporting logic through Chwezi. Use Design System Skills and document/spreadsheet tooling for rendered or workbook outputs. Load only sector, country, funding, valuation, M&E, and execution skills triggered by the product.

### Authority and evidence rules

Project-local reversible edits are authorised. Do not contact customers, launch tests publicly, submit funding applications, change live models, move money, publish claims, or alter canonical engines without separate authority. Never invent market size, demand, customers, benchmarks, exchange rates, tax, regulation, traction, team credentials, or financial evidence. Label every material statement as verified fact, management input, assumption, estimate, inference, hypothesis, or unknown. Missing evidence is `NOT ASSESSED`, not a pass.

### Create the cycle record

Create `docs/kaizen/<cycle-id>/` with the seven standard files: `00-scope-and-evidence.md`, `01-baseline-scorecard.md`, `02-improvement-backlog.md`, `03-experiment-log.md`, `04-validation-record.md`, `05-final-report.md`, and `06-next-cycle.md`. Inventory the decision, audience, sources, claim register, assumptions, customer evidence, market model, operating plan, financial workbook, scenarios, funding ask, implementation plan, risks, governance, reviewers, and release bundle.

### Freeze the capped baseline

Score these ten equal dimensions with named evidence, confidence, deficiency, and status:

1. Decision thesis, scope, audience fit, and clear recommendation.
2. Customer or beneficiary problem, segmentation, demand evidence, and alternatives.
3. Market, sector, country, competitor, regulatory, and source quality.
4. Business or impact model, value creation, revenue/funding logic, and unit economics.
5. Go-to-market, pricing, channels, partnerships, retention, and measurable learning.
6. Operations, capacity, technology, supply chain, quality, and implementation feasibility.
7. Team, governance, decision rights, stakeholders, ethics, and execution ownership.
8. Financial reconciliation, cash, working capital, scenarios, funding need, use of funds, and accounting review.
9. Risks, sensitivities, countercases, continuity, controls, and stop/pivot rules.
10. Milestones, M&E, KPI definitions, cadence, document/model fidelity, release evidence, and living-plan refresh.

Show raw dimension scores and calculate `published_baseline = min(raw_overall, 65)`. Freeze it before changes. Bankability, compliance, affordability, achievability, and investor readiness remain blocked when their required evidence or professional review is absent.

### Plan the route to 95

Rank a P0/P1/P2 backlog by decision impact, evidence weakness, cash or mission risk, feasibility, and reversibility. Each item needs gap, evidence, root cause, exact artefact, hypothesis, owner, due date, primary measure, counter-metric, smallest ethical test, risk, rollback/stop rule, acceptance proof, financial/model effect, target contribution, and re-audit date. Expose what must stop, narrow, or be sequenced; do not solve overload by adding initiatives.

### Run controlled improvements

Work one hypothesis at a time. Examples include repairing an assumption ledger, reconciling a model schedule, narrowing an unsupported claim, strengthening a customer test design, aligning capacity with sales, correcting the funding ask, adding a downside case, or connecting a KPI to a decision threshold. Preserve before/after evidence and rerun every affected narrative, model, source, and release check. If a result improves enquiries but harms fulfilment, cash, mission, trust, or compliance, classify it as mixed or failed and do not standardise it.

Where real-world validation requires external contact or elapsed time, prepare the test instrument, baseline, sample, threshold, counter-metric, owner, and decision rule, but mark the outcome pending. Do not fabricate a result to finish the cycle.

### Strict anti-AI-slop gate

Apply the engine's anti-AI-slop skill while writing and run its audit after every major section/iteration and at release. Grade F blocks release. Reject invented market sizes, growth rates, customers, quotes, prices, traction, team credentials, sources, benchmarks, model results, and validation outcomes; generic SWOT/PESTLE filler; laundry-list strategies; unsupported “huge market” claims; hockey-stick forecasts; uniform paragraph patterns; vague premium language; consultant cliches; duplicated section summaries; decorative frameworks with no decision; and recommendations that ignore capacity, cash, governance, downside, or implementation.

Every load-bearing statement must expose claim, evidence, warrant, assumption, countercase, and implication. Every number must trace to a source or labelled model assumption. Replace generic prose with decision-specific analysis, quantified drivers, real trade-offs, named owners, thresholds, and consequences. A polished document or balanced-looking workbook does not pass if the underlying evidence or business logic is synthetic.

### Validate the integrated plan

Run applicable source-register, sector/regulatory, anti-slop, critical-logic, model, workbook, scenario, reconciliation, release-bundle, document render, accessibility, and reviewer gates. Confirm that:

- claims link to sources and warrants, with assumptions and countercases visible;
- customer, market, revenue, operating, staffing, and funding logic reconcile;
- income statement, cash flow, balance sheet, working capital, scenarios, and use of funds reconcile where applicable;
- downside, liquidity, capacity, covenant, impact, and implementation risks have decision rules;
- objectives and KPIs have definitions, baselines, owners, cadence, thresholds, and actions;
- the audience can make the intended decision from the final artefact;
- native spreadsheet, DOCX, PDF, or presentation outputs are opened, rendered, and inspected rather than inferred from source text.

Record exact commands, reviewer evidence, calculations, output paths, failures, and unavailable checks.

### Standardise and re-measure

Update the project's assumption ledger, source register, model, decision log, risk register, test record, template, operating plan, or release bundle with accepted learning. Re-score using only new evidence. Report the uncapped final score and do not claim 95 unless every score and blocker supports it. Keep pending field tests and professional reviews explicit.

Write an answer-first final report with the decision implication, before/after scores, implemented changes, model effects, validation results, blockers, residual risks, and release verdict. Add the next experiment and refresh trigger. Propose engine learning only when it is repeatable across engagements; do not edit the canonical engine.

### Final response

Return the published and raw baseline, evidenced final score, decision/release verdict, completed improvements, model and evidence changes, validations, pending or failed experiments, blockers, evidence-pack path, and next review date. Never describe the plan as bankable, achievable, compliant, or submission-ready beyond the evidence.
