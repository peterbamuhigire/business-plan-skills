## Synthesis admitted into the business-plan engine

1. Treat growth as a sequence of hypotheses and experiments, with reversible tests, guardrails, owners, and review dates.
2. Put the customer’s observed problem and decision context before the company’s self-description.
3. Reduce choice friction while preserving material alternatives, price, terms, and risk information.
4. Treat proof, mechanism, and risk control as the foundation of premium positioning.
5. Use sensory, ritual, and identity cues to improve recognition and service consistency, never as unsupported neuroscience claims.
6. Use copywriting craft to clarify a sound business model; never use it to hide weak economics.
